Made for matchday.

A fan-made exhibition game, with fictional teams. Player appearances are community-created models; no player, club or league endorsement is implied. Gameplay ratings are tuned for this game, not official statistics.

Player models

  • Cristiano Ronaldo Rigged by lolohno75, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Adapted proportions, orientation, animation, kit colors, mesh detail and compression. Its boot mesh is also fitted to Messi and the supporting squad.
  • 3D Lionel Messi Barca Jersey by caonghia2009, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Adapted bind pose, proportions, animation, kits, socks, footwear, mesh detail and compression.
  • Supporting athletes and officials: Man Player by RiverofCreative, CC BY 4.0. Adapted rig, adult builds, face geometry, kits, socks, football boots, animation and mesh detail.
  • Football and walking motion: Adobe Mixamo. Walking motion is supplied by the official Three.js Soldier animation example. Distributed as part of the playable game.

Stadium and atmosphere

The stadium is an original procedural design, informed by the supplied Al Wakrah visual reference. It is not an exact reconstruction of Al Janoub and contains no geometry from that Sketchfab stadium.

Daylight sky: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Puresky by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. Turf, concrete, environment, recorded crowd and ball audio retain their original credits.
