Touch buttons explained

Joystick
Move your player and aim your pass.
Pass
Send the ball to a teammate.
Shoot
Kick at goal. Hold for more power; release to kick.
Through
Pass ahead of a running teammate.
Lob
Lift a pass over defenders.
Sprint
Hold while moving to run faster.
Switch
Take control of another defender.
Tackle
Try to win the ball from the opponent.
Defend
Hold to slow down and face the attacker.
Help press
Tap to send a teammate to pressure the ball. Tap again to stop.
Slide
Slide towards the ball. Poor timing can cause a foul.

Choose your kick style

Tap the style button, or press Q, to cycle through these options. Then use PASS or SHOOT.

Normal
Ground pass or straight shot.
Lob
Lifted pass; shooting stays normal.
Curve (finesse)
Curved shot; passing stays normal.
Chip
High shot over the keeper; passing stays normal.

Touch gestures

Hold PASS or SHOOT for power; release to kick. Swipe PASS up for a lob, down to pass and run forward. Swipe THROUGH up for a lofted through ball. Swipe SHOOT down for a curved shot, up for a chip. Attack buttons switch to defending actions when your team loses the ball.
