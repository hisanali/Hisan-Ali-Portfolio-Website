    Questions, answered

    What are UTM parameters?

    Small tags added to a URL that tell Google Analytics where a visitor came from — for example, which ad, email or post they clicked.

    Why lowercase everything?

    GA4 treats “Facebook” and “facebook” as different sources. Consistent lowercase names keep your reports clean.

    Which medium should I use for social ads?

    Use paid_social for paid campaigns and social for organic posts, so GA4 puts the traffic in the right default channel.

    Should I add UTMs to links on my own site?

    No. UTMs on internal links start a new session and overwrite the visitor’s original traffic source.
