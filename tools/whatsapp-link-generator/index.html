Questions, answered

      
Does this work with WhatsApp Business?
Yes. wa.me links work with both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers.
Why must I drop the leading zero?
WhatsApp links need the full international number without “+”, spaces or the local leading 0 — this tool formats it for you.
Will the link ever stop working?
No. It’s WhatsApp’s official click-to-chat format, so it works as long as the number is on WhatsApp.
Where should I use the link?
Instagram and TikTok bios, Google Business Profile, ads, email signatures and your website — anywhere customers might want to message you.
