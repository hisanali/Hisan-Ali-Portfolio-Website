          How it works

          1. Paste or writeYour draft is analysed as you type and autosaved in this browser.
          2. Check readabilityAim for a Flesch score of 60+ for web copy. Split the long sentences the tool highlights.
          3. Tune your keywordsSet a focus keyword to check usage and density, and review the phrases you repeat most.

          Questions

          Is my text sent anywhere?

          No. All analysis runs in your browser. The autosaved draft is stored only on this device.

          What is a good readability score?

          60–70 is plain English and suits most websites. Technical content can be lower; social captions should be higher.

          What keyword density should I aim for?

          There’s no magic number. Use your main phrase naturally — usually 0.5–2%. Above 3% often reads as stuffing.

          Does it work for Arabic or other languages?

          Word, character, sentence and keyword counts work for any language. The readability score is designed for English.
