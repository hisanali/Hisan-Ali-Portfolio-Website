          Questions, answered

          Is my text sent anywhere?

          No. All analysis runs in your browser. The autosaved draft is stored only on this device.

          What’s a good readability score?

          60–70 is plain English and suits most websites. Technical content can score lower; social captions should score higher.

          What keyword density should I aim for?

          There’s no magic number. Use your main phrase naturally — usually 0.5–2%. Above 3% often reads as keyword stuffing.

          Does it work in Arabic or other languages?

          Word, character, sentence and keyword counts work in any language. The readability score is designed for English.
