Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used on this website, and what choices you have.

Last updated: March 11, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember certain preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

2. How Cookies May Be Used

This website may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: to support basic website operation and security.
  • Analytics: to understand how visitors use the website and which pages or features perform best.
  • Performance measurement: to evaluate site speed, usability, and general visitor experience.
  • Preferences: to remember basic settings or improve how the website behaves where applicable.

3. Analytics and Performance Tools

This website uses Vercel Analytics to better understand visitor behavior and site performance. This may involve cookies or similar technologies that help measure:

  • pages visited.
  • browser and device type.
  • traffic sources.
  • general usage patterns.
  • approximate location data.

This information is used to improve website content, usability, and performance.

4. Third-Party Cookies and Technologies

Some third-party services used by this website may set or rely on their own cookies or similar technologies. These services may include analytics, hosting-related tools, or performance features.

Any data handled by these third-party services is processed according to their own policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the website function.

Browser settings usually allow you to:

  • view stored cookies.
  • block all cookies.
  • delete existing cookies.
  • limit cookies from specific websites.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, analytics, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.
