Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Last updated: March 11, 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Hisan Ali and provides information about digital marketing services, blog content, and tools such as the page speed test.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact: workhisan@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact information submitted through forms, such as your name, email address, and message content.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, browser type, device type, and approximate location.
  • Technical data related to website access, performance, and security.
  • URLs submitted through the page speed test tool, along with related performance and timing results.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and form submissions.
  • Improve website content, performance, and user experience.
  • Monitor site traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Generate performance reports through the page speed test tool.

4. Contact Forms

When you submit information through a contact form, the details you provide may be stored and processed through Google Sheets for inquiry handling and follow-up.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through general contact forms unless specifically requested.

5. Analytics

This website uses Vercel Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site and to measure performance. This may include data such as:

  • Pages visited.
  • Device and browser type.
  • General usage patterns.
  • Approximate location data.
  • Referring pages or sources.

This information helps improve the website and understand visitor behavior.

6. Page Speed Test Tool

This website may include a page speed test feature powered by the Google PageSpeed Insights API. When you use this tool, the URL you submit is processed in order to generate performance-related results.

Submitted URLs and related performance data may be handled temporarily for tool functionality and result display.

7. Hosting and Infrastructure

This website is hosted on Vercel. As part of hosting, delivery, analytics, and security, Vercel may process technical information such as IP address, request logs, browser details, and device-related data.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support analytics, performance measurement, and general site functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some parts of the website may not function as expected if cookies are restricted.

9. Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information.

However, limited data may be processed by third-party services used to operate the website, including:

  • Vercel for hosting and analytics.
  • Google Sheets for handling contact form submissions.
  • Google PageSpeed Insights API for page speed testing.

These services process information only as needed to support website functionality, analytics, communication, or performance testing.

10. Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • respond to inquiries.
  • operate and improve the website.
  • maintain records where needed.
  • meet legal, technical, or operational obligations.

11. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information handled through the website. However, no website or online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information submitted through this website.

To make a privacy-related request, contact: workhisan@gmail.com

13. External Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are independent and are not covered by this Privacy Policy.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.

15. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: workhisan@gmail.com
