Saudi Arabia Marketing Calendar 2027

Every date that moves demand in Saudi Arabia in 2027, in order, with what each one means for your campaigns.

Updated October 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali
2027 is unusual: Ramadan is expected to start around 8 February, so several February moments across the GCC fall inside the holy month. Plan evening-led, respectful campaigns.

01Key dates in order

DateMomentWhat it means
~8 FebRamadan begins (expected)Evenings become prime time; food, gifting and charity rise.
22 FebFounding DayA national celebration of the state’s founding; in 2027 it falls during Ramadan, so tone matters.
~10 MarEid al-Fitr (expected)Peak for fashion, gifts, sweets, salons and short trips.
~15–16 MayDay of Arafah and Eid al-Adha (expected)Travel, family gatherings and gifting.
~6 JunIslamic New Year 1449 (expected)Public holiday as summer begins.

Late NovWhite Friday and 11.11Big online sales moments.

“~” marks expected Islamic dates, confirmed by moon sighting. Official days off are announced by each government and may be moved.

02The year by quarter

Q1Jan–MarRamadan (expected from ~8 February) and Eid al-Fitr (~10 March) dominate. Move ad schedules to evenings, plan Eid delivery cut-offs, and treat national days that fall in Ramadan with a calmer tone.
Q2Apr–JunA post-Eid reset in April, then Eid al-Adha (~16 May) and the start of summer. Good months for retention, reviews and testing new offers.
Q3Jul–SepSummer travel lowers activity in many cities; back to school in late August or September brings a sharp spike for retail, education and transport.
Q4Oct–DecPleasant weather, events and the November sales cluster (11.11 and White Friday), followed by national celebrations and year-end budgets.

Questions

When is Ramadan 2027 in Saudi Arabia?

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid al-Fitr around 10 March 2027, subject to the official moon sighting.

What are the biggest marketing moments in Saudi Arabia in 2027?

Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr in Q1, Eid al-Adha in May, back to school in late summer, and the November and December cluster of sales and national celebrations.

How early should I plan GCC campaigns?

Plan Ramadan campaigns three to four months ahead, and other peaks about two to three months ahead, warming up audiences four to six weeks before each one.
