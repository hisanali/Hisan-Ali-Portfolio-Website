# Tideworn environmental audio

All recordings are **[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)**. Credits are retained as a courtesy to the recordists. Source license declarations were checked on 15 November 2026.

Loop edits use conservative filtering, constant gain and a 3-second cyclic crossfade. Freesound excerpts use the publicly available HQ MP3 previews. No music or synthesized ambient beds are included.

Woodland and stream recordings may include incidental wildlife. Rain was recorded at a window and processed to stereo by its author.
