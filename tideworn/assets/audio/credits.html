Environmental audio credits

Real environmental recordings and physical sound effects used in Tideworn.

All source recordings below are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. We credit the recordists for their work. Original license declarations were verified on 15 November 2026.

The excerpts are trimmed, lightly filtered and level-adjusted. Ambient loops use a three-second crossfade. Freesound assets use public HQ MP3 previews; OpenGameArt assets use their original downloads. No music is included.
