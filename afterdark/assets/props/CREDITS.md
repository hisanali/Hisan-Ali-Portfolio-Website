# Afterdark props

All models in this folder are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under
[CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) (public domain; no attribution required, credited here with thanks).
They were downloaded at 1k texture resolution and optimised for the web with glTF-Transform
(Draco geometry, WebP textures, simplified meshes where the scans were very dense).
