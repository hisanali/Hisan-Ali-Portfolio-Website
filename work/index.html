01 · Brand ecosystem

Bella Bathwares

A premium catalogue website designed to make a broad product range easier to explore and easier to enquire about.

Project typeCatalogue website
Primary focusDiscovery & enquiry
AudienceRetail & trade buyers
Live evidencebellabathwares.com ↗
The brief

Make a large catalogue feel premium without making customers work harder.

Bella serves several markets and carries a wide bathware range. The website needed to support product discovery, brand credibility, showroom awareness, and direct enquiries within one clear experience.

Work delivered
  • Information and category architecture
  • Homepage and catalogue UX direction
  • Premium visual system
  • Lead and showroom pathways
  • Responsive website experience
What to inspect
  • Persistent product-category navigation
  • Clear collection and showroom routes
  • Product-first page hierarchy
  • Visible enquiry opportunities
  • Consistent multi-market presentation
Inspect the live website ↗
01 · Discover

Map the buying paths.

Separate product browsing, collection discovery, showroom research, and direct enquiry so each visitor has an obvious next step.

02 · Structure

Organize the catalogue.

Give product families and collections a repeatable hierarchy that stays understandable as the range expands.

03 · Express

Build premium restraint.

Use typography, space, product imagery, and controlled contrast to support quality without overwhelming the catalogue.

04 · Convert

Keep action visible.

Connect product interest to enquiries and showroom routes without turning every page into a hard-sell landing page.
