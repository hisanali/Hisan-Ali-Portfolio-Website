01 · Brand ecosystem

Bella Bathwares

Website direction and an ongoing creative system for a multi-market bathware brand.

The constraint

A large product catalogue needed to feel premium without making discovery harder.

The brand serves different audiences and markets. The digital experience had to hold product breadth, showroom credibility, and campaign communication together.

The decisions
  • Clarified category and collection paths
  • Built a calmer premium visual direction
  • Connected campaign creative with the website brand
  • Kept enquiry and showroom discovery visible
The inspectable outcome

A consistent customer-facing system across the catalogue website and recurring campaign work. No private commercial metrics are published without client approval.

Oman Search Intent Study

A practical content asset designed around how people in Oman research before buying.

The problem

Generic keyword lists do not explain the customer decision behind a search.

The approach

Queries were organized by intent and decision stage, then translated into practical content and campaign implications for Oman businesses.

The output

An indexable, shareable research article that demonstrates local market thinking rather than repeating global marketing advice.
