01 The story

How much time have you got?

  1. Who. A Muscat-based digital marketer with 5+ years of hands-on work across Oman and the GCC.
  2. What. SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, analytics and creative — run as one system instead of five separate jobs.
  3. Why me. Direct communication, practical priorities, and reporting you can actually make decisions with.

I got into marketing through the work itself — building campaigns, designing creative, fixing tracking, and writing the pages people land on.

That range turned out to be the useful part. When you have run the ads, made the visuals and read the analytics, you stop seeing “SEO” and “social” as separate projects. You see one customer, one journey, and a few places where it quietly leaks.

Most businesses I meet in Oman don’t need more activity. They need someone to find those few leaks, fix them in the right order, and explain what changed in plain language. That is the job I enjoy most.

Outside client work I publish field notes on Oman search and advertising, run original studies on how people here search before buying, and build free tools in the Lab — mostly because I can’t leave a good problem alone.

“Replace random marketing activity with a focused system for visibility, demand and long-term growth.”

04 Opinions, held firmly

Six things I believe about marketing.

Tap any card for the reasoning. If you nod along to most of them, we’ll probably work well together.

  1. Most “bad leads” are slow replies. A simple response system — who answers, how fast, with what — often lifts sales more than a bigger ad budget.

    The lead response system

  2. People who can’t see a price usually scroll on to a competitor who shows one. Clear pricing, or at least a range, qualifies buyers before they message.

    Why hidden prices cost sales

  3. For local businesses, more people see your Maps listing than your website. Categories, photos and reviews there decide who gets the call.

    Rank higher on Maps in Oman

  4. Broken tracking makes bad campaigns look good and good ones look bad. Fixing measurement first is the cheapest optimisation there is.

    Fix your conversion tracking

  5. A promotion should be run through margin and break-even volume before launch. Busy is not the same as profitable.

    The discount profit test

  6. Ad accounts, analytics, domains and pages belong to your business. I work with access — never as the only person holding the keys.

    Run the ownership audit
