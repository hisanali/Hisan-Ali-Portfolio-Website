11.11 Falls on a Wednesday This Year.
How Oman Shops Can Use It Without Wrecking White Friday

Singles’ Day, 11 November, has become the first big online sale of the Gulf’s November. In past years Amazon.ae has opened its 11.11 sale to shoppers in Oman and noon has run its own. This year it lands on Wednesday 11 November, two weeks before White Friday on 27 November and close to National Day. How to plan three moments in one month, and a calculator for whether a discount still makes money.

Updated Nov 3, 202611.11 is Wednesday 11 November 2026
November 3, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Shoppers in Oman have learned that November is when prices fall. Big marketplaces start with 11.11, keep going through White Friday, and many local shops join late with a deep discount that eats their margin. A better plan gives each moment its own job: 11.11 to warm up the audience, National Day for local pride and gifting, and White Friday for the strongest offer.

Tips for a calm November

  • Get approval early for each promotion; see the White Friday profit plan.

  • Ask for prepayment by QR or transfer to cut cash-on-delivery returns.

Common questions

When is 11.11 in 2026?

Singles’ Day falls on Wednesday 11 November 2026. Marketplace sales often start a few days earlier and run a day or two after.

Do Amazon and noon run 11.11 sales for Oman?

In past years Amazon.ae opened its 11.11 sale to customers in Oman, with prices in Omani rials, and noon ran an 11.11 sale in the Gulf. Check their sites for 2026 dates and whether Oman is included.

Should a small shop in Oman join 11.11?

Yes, with a small offer on a few items or a gift for joining your customer list. Keep the deepest discount for White Friday on 27 November.

Do November promotions in Oman need approval?

Yes. Promotions and discounts generally need approval from the Consumer Protection Authority before they are advertised, so apply early.
