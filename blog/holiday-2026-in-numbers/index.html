Holiday 2026 in Numbers:
$275 Billion, and AI Traffic Up 130%

Adobe forecasts a record $275.1 billion in US online holiday sales this year, with Cyber Monday the biggest day ever and traffic from AI assistants more than doubling. The numbers that matter, what they mean for any online store, and a planner that turns them into targets for your own peak days.

Key takeaways
  • Cyber Week (Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday) is forecast at $47.5 billion, about 17% of the whole season in five days.
  • Mobile is expected to take 57.4% of online spend.
  • Traffic from AI assistants to retail sites is forecast to grow 130%; it is still small, but it is the fastest-growing source.
  • Social (+17%) and affiliates (+12%) are expected to grow much faster than paid search (+4%).

Checked 25 October 2026. Figures are Adobe Digital Insights’ US forecast, published 28 September 2026 and based on visits to US retail sites. They are forecasts, not results. Other markets differ, but the pattern of a short, mobile-heavy peak is now global.

Five days carry about one sixth of the season. A store that is slow, out of stock or under-budgeted that week loses sales it cannot recover in December.

03The AI shopper

Adobe measures AI traffic as clicks to retail sites from AI chat services and AI browsers. A 130% rise is a growth rate, not a share: AI is still a small slice of total visits. But this shopper arrives later in the decision, having already compared options.1

Asks

“Best gift under $50 for a runner”

AI answers with a shortlist built from product data, reviews and prices it can read.

Compares

Price, stock, delivery, returns

Missing shipping times or return policies make a product easy to drop from the list.

Clicks

Arrives ready to buy

AI visitors usually land on a product page, not the home page. That page has to close the sale.

04Five moves for the next 30 days

1

Fix mobile checkout first

With 57% of spend on phones, test checkout on a mid-range phone on mobile data. Remove every step you can.

2

Load budget into Cyber Week

Plan daily budgets for 26–30 November now, and agree who can raise them on the day.

3

Clean your product feed

Prices, stock, shipping and returns feed Shopping ads and AI answers. See Universal Cart readiness.

4

Brief creators and affiliates

Social and affiliates are growing fastest. Give partners codes, assets and dates two weeks ahead.

5

Protect your margin

Decide the deepest discount you can afford before the noise starts. Plan the profit before the discount.

Common questions

How much will US shoppers spend online in holiday 2026?

Adobe forecasts $275.1 billion in US online sales from 1 November to 31 December 2026, up 6.7% on 2025.

What will be the biggest online shopping day of 2026?

Cyber Monday, 30 November 2026, forecast by Adobe at $15.1 billion, up 6.2% year on year. Cyber Week, from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday, is forecast at $47.5 billion.

How much will AI drive holiday shopping in 2026?

Adobe forecasts that traffic to US retail sites from AI services, such as AI chat assistants and AI browsers, will grow 130% year on year this holiday season. It is a growth rate; AI is still a small share of total visits.

Which marketing channels will grow fastest this holiday season?

Adobe forecasts social media to grow 17% and affiliates 12% as sources of online sales, compared with 4% for paid search, which remains the largest paid channel.
