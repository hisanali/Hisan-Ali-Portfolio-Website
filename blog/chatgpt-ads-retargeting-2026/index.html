ChatGPT Ads · New Capability

People Who See Your ChatGPT Ad Can Now Be Shown Your Ad on TV.
How It Works and When to Use It

On 27 October, StackAdapt, one of the ad platforms that sells ChatGPT ads, said advertisers can now retarget people who saw their ChatGPT ads on connected TV, display and its other channels. Until now, a ChatGPT ad lived and died inside the chat. What has changed, what is still unclear, and a planner for a simple sequence from chat to screen to sale.

Updated Nov 3, 2026Announced 27 October 2026
November 3, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Retargeting is the oldest trick in online advertising: someone shows interest, and you remind them later. ChatGPT ads were the exception. Someone could see your ad while asking about a product and you had no way to follow up, unless they clicked. StackAdapt’s update changes that for advertisers buying through it. Its Data Hub now builds an audience of people who saw a ChatGPT ad and lets you reach them elsewhere.

27 OctStackAdapt announces ChatGPT viewer retargeting
3+channels named: connected TV, display and other programmatic inventory
May 2026when StackAdapt began selling ChatGPT ads in OpenAI’s pilot
In short
  • Advertisers buying ChatGPT ads through StackAdapt can retarget the people who saw them.
  • Follow-up ads can run on connected TV, display and StackAdapt’s other inventory.
  • StackAdapt has not said how a viewer is matched across channels, or in which countries it works.
  • Use it for considered purchases where a chat is the start of research, not the end.

How the sequence works

A view in ChatGPT is a strong signal: the person was actively asking about something your ad relates to. That is closer to a search than a scroll. Following it with a brand spot on TV builds trust, and a display ad with a clear offer gives them a way to act.

Before you try it

  • Keep the message consistent. The same product, promise and look in chat, on TV and in the banner.
  • Cap frequency. People who asked a question once should not see you twenty times.
  • Measure lift, not last click. Compare sales from people in the sequence with a holdout group that only saw the chat ad.
  • Check availability for your country before you plan around it; ChatGPT ads are still rolling out market by market.

Common questions

Can you retarget people who saw a ChatGPT ad?

Yes, if you buy ChatGPT ads through StackAdapt. On 27 October 2026 it announced that advertisers can retarget ChatGPT ad viewers across connected TV, display and its other programmatic channels.

How does ChatGPT ad retargeting work?

StackAdapt builds an audience of people who saw your ChatGPT ad in its Data Hub and uses it in campaigns on other channels. It has not disclosed how viewers are matched across devices or channels.

Is ChatGPT ad retargeting available in Oman?

The announcement does not list countries. Ask StackAdapt or your agency whether ChatGPT ads and viewer retargeting are available for the market you want to reach.

Which businesses should use it?

Businesses with considered purchases, such as cars, property, education, travel, electronics and professional services, where a question in ChatGPT is the start of research and a follow-up can bring the person back.
