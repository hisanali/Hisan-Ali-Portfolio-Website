Ads Inside WhatsApp Status:
The 24-Hour Placement Most Brands Haven’t Tried

WhatsApp now runs ads between people’s Status updates and promotes Channels in the Updates tab, bought through Meta Ads Manager like any other placement. Chats stay ad-free. What the placement is, how it behaves, the creative rules that matter on a full-screen, sound-off, tap-to-skip format, and a safe-zone checker for your 9:16 creative.

Key takeaways
  • Status ads appear between Status updates in the Updates tab; Promoted Channels appear in Channel discovery. Chats stay encrypted and ad-free.
  • They are bought in Meta Ads Manager as a placement, with WhatsApp chat, your site or a Channel as the destination.

01What the placement is

Two things arrived together: ads in Status, shown as full-screen stories between the updates people already watch, and Promoted Channels, which surface a Channel in the directory. Both live only in the Updates tab. The mindset row matters most. People open Status to see friends and family. An ad that feels like a shop window interrupts; an ad that feels like a message from someone they know gets a reply. Chat as the destination fits that mood. See what a WhatsApp reply now costs before you route everyone into chat.

03Creative rules

Frame

Respect the overlays

The interface covers roughly the top and bottom of the frame: progress bars and name on top, reply bar and CTA at the bottom. Keep logo, headline and offer in the middle.

Time

Win the first second

The thumb is already on the screen. Open with the product or the offer, not a logo animation.

Sound

Design for silence

Captions or on-screen text carry the message. Sound is a bonus, not the plan.

Tone

Look like a Status

Handheld, vertical, a person talking: it reads as an update, not an ad. Polished horizontal cut-downs read as intrusion.

Length

Short, then shorter

Guides cite up to 30 seconds for ads; the ones that finish are far shorter. Say it in 6–10 seconds.

Action

One clear next step

“Chat on WhatsApp” with a prepared first message converts better than a vague “Learn more”.

04A first test

1

Check availability

In Ads Manager, create a campaign and look for WhatsApp Status under placements. If it is missing, your market or objective is not live yet.

2

Start with chat as the destination

Use a click-to-WhatsApp objective with a prepared first message, so the reply starts the conversation for the customer.

3

Make two native creatives

One person-to-camera, one product-first, both vertical, captioned, under 10 seconds. Test them against each other, not against your Reels ad.

4

Judge by conversations, not views

Count started chats, replies within an hour and orders. A cheap view that nobody answers is not a result.

Status is where people check on the people they care about. Earn your place in that list.

Common questions

Does WhatsApp show ads now?

Yes, only in the Updates tab. Since February 2026 WhatsApp has rolled out ads between Status updates and Promoted Channels worldwide, with availability still phased by market and account. Personal chats and calls remain end-to-end encrypted and ad-free.

How do I buy WhatsApp Status ads?

Through Meta Ads Manager. Select WhatsApp Status as a placement, upload a vertical 9:16 creative, set your audience and budget, and choose a destination such as a WhatsApp chat, your website or a Channel. There is no separate WhatsApp ad console.

What size should a WhatsApp Status ad be?

Full-screen vertical 9:16, with 1080 × 1920 pixels recommended, as an image (JPG/PNG) or video (MP4/MOV). Keep key elements in the middle of the frame, away from the interface at the top and bottom.

How much do WhatsApp Status ads cost?

There is no published rate. Status ads and Promoted Channels are auction inventory priced like other Meta placements, depending on audience, competition and objective.
