Snapchat · Earnings Decoded

Snapchat’s Ad Revenue Grew Just 4%.
Its Users Outside the West Grew 9%. What That Means for Gulf Brands

Snap grew revenue 14% to .72 billion in the third quarter, but advertising grew only about 4% and subscriptions supplied around three-quarters of the growth. Daily users reached 495 million: down 6% in North America, up 9% in the rest of the world, which includes the Gulf. Sponsored Snaps, ads in the chat inbox, grew 56% on the quarter. What it means for advertisers, and a planner for Snapchat in the GCC.

Updated Nov 12, 2026Q3 results 4 November 2026
November 12, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Snap’s chief executive Evan Spiegel called the quarter’s ad growth “below our expectations”, with a fall in upper-funnel brand advertising. But for advertisers in Oman, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, where Snapchat is one of the most used apps, the more useful number is where users are growing: almost entirely outside North America and Europe.

In short
  • Subscriptions (Snapchat+, storage, Lens+) passed 30 million and drove about 74% of revenue growth.
  • Sponsored Snaps revenue grew 56% from the previous quarter.
  • From 2027 Snap will report monthly users (977 million now) instead of daily users.
  • For Gulf brands: Snapchat’s audience is strongest where you sell. Test Sponsored Snaps and lead ads before White Friday.

Common questions

How did Snap perform in Q3 2026?

Revenue rose 14% to $1.72 billion. Advertising grew about 4%, while subscription and other revenue rose 84% to $349.7 million. Daily active users reached 495 million.

Where are Snapchat’s users growing?

Daily users in the rest of the world, which includes the Middle East, rose 9% to 305 million, while North America fell 6% and Europe fell 1%.

What are Sponsored Snaps?

Ads delivered in the Snapchat chat inbox, which people open like a message. Snap said Sponsored Snaps revenue grew 56% from the second quarter.

Is Snapchat worth advertising on in Oman?

Snapchat is widely used in the Gulf, especially by younger audiences. It suits fashion, beauty, food, entertainment, retail and launches. Test it with proper conversion tracking and native vertical video.
