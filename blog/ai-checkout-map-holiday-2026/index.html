Which AI Can Buy From Your Store?
The AI Checkout Map for Holiday 2026

Google’s AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Meta’s Muse and Perplexity can now complete purchases inside the chat. ChatGPT, surprisingly, cannot: it handed checkout back to merchants in March. Who supports what, through which payment rails, and a checker that shows which AI checkouts can already reach your store.

Key takeaways
  • Google AI Mode and Gemini check out through Google Pay and the Universal Commerce Protocol, with Universal Cart.
  • Copilot uses PayPal, Shopify and Stripe; Perplexity uses PayPal; Meta’s Muse uses Shop Pay and Stripe Link.
  • ChatGPT now sends shoppers to your own checkout or a retailer app, after ending Instant Checkout in March.
  • Your store platform and payment provider largely decide which AI checkouts you can join. Most are US only so far.

ChatGPT

Why it stepped back

OpenAI ended Instant Checkout on 24 March 2026, choosing to send shoppers to merchants’ own checkout and retailer apps instead. Discovery inside ChatGPT continues.3

Meta Muse

The newest entrant

Muse can browse, choose and check out, but asks for approval before paying. Shopify’s deal brings Shop Pay checkout across Shopify stores; Amazon has blocked Muse.4

Perplexity

PayPal powered

Instant Buy launched with PayPal in November 2025 for US users. Merchants that are not compatible are still shown, but shoppers finish on their site.5

Amazon

A walled garden

Amazon lets its own assistant buy on Amazon and blocks outside agents. Walmart has reportedly opened a gateway for other agents.1

Common questions

Can ChatGPT buy products for shoppers?

Not inside the chat any more. OpenAI ended Instant Checkout in March 2026. ChatGPT still recommends products, then sends shoppers to the merchant’s own checkout or a retailer app inside ChatGPT.

Which AI assistants can check out inside the chat?

As of October 2026, reports list Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Meta’s Muse and Perplexity as able to complete purchases inside the conversation, mostly for US shoppers.

How do merchants get into AI checkout?

Mostly through the platforms they already use: Google Merchant Center for AI Mode and Gemini, PayPal for Perplexity and Copilot, Shopify and Stripe for Copilot and Meta’s Muse. Each sets its own eligibility rules.

Is AI checkout available outside the US?

Mostly not yet. Most in-chat checkouts launched in the US first. AI product discovery works more widely, so accurate product data matters everywhere.
