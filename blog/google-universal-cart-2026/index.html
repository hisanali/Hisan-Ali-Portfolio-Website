Google’s Universal Cart:
One Cart for Every Store, Weeks Before Black Friday

On 15 October 2026, Google launched Universal Cart in Search, AI Mode and the Gemini app: one cart that holds products from many retailers, watches their prices and can check out on Google or hand the cart to your site. How it works, who can use it and a feed check to make sure your products can be added.

Key takeaways
  • Shoppers can add products from different retailers to one Google cart, then get alerts on deals and price drops.
  • Checkout happens on Google (Google Pay) or by handing the cart to your website. Either way, you stay the merchant of record.
  • It runs on Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), switched on in Merchant Center’s UCP hub, rolling out gradually in the US first.
  • Everywhere else, the lesson is the same: the cart reads your price, stock and shipping data from your feed. Clean feeds win.

01What Universal Cart does

Until now, shopping on Google ended with a click to a store. Universal Cart keeps the basket on Google. A shopper can add a jacket from one retailer, shoes from another and a bag from a third, wherever they find them in Search, AI Mode or the Gemini app.12

Cart

One basket, many stores

Items from different retailers sit together. The shopper compares, removes and decides in one place, without opening your site.

Watch

Deals and price drops

While items wait in the cart, Google monitors them for deals and price changes and alerts the shopper. Reports also mention restock alerts and promo codes.3

Pay

Checkout on Google

Where supported, shoppers pay with Google Pay without leaving Google. The order is still yours: you are the merchant of record.

Hand-off

Or continue on your site

Merchants can instead receive the whole cart on their own site, where the shopper finishes checkout as usual.

02How a cart reaches your store

Universal Cart is built on the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), Google’s open standard that lets AI agents and shopping surfaces talk to merchants’ systems. Merchants choose what to switch on in the UCP integration hub in Merchant Center.4

1Shopper adds your product in Search, AI Mode or Gemini
2Google reads price, stock and shipping from your Merchant Center feed
3Cart waits; Google watches for price drops and deals
4Native checkout on Google with Google Pay

03Who can use it, and when

United StatesLive for shoppers since 15 October; UCP hub rolling out gradually to merchants.
Canada & AustraliaNext, according to Google’s help page, from next year.
UK, Europe, GCC & othersNo date announced.

Reported launch partners include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta, Walmart, Wayfair and many Shopify merchants.2 Google previewed YouTube and Gmail as future places to add items, but they were not part of the October launch.6

Not in the US? You cannot switch Universal Cart on yet, but the same feed data already decides whether your products show in Shopping ads, free listings and AI Mode answers in your market. Fixing it now pays twice. See how AI shopping agents choose products.

04What to do before Black Friday

This week

Fix price and availability mismatches. A cart that shows the wrong price at checkout loses the sale and can get products disapproved.

Next week

Complete shipping, returns and GTINs. These fill the details a shopper compares when your product sits next to a competitor’s.

By 15 Nov

Submit Black Friday promotions and sale prices with start and end dates, so price-drop alerts fire for your products.

US merchants

Check the UCP hub in Merchant Center. If cart transfer is available, test it in the sandbox: it keeps checkout, upsells and data on your site.

Common questions

What is Google Universal Cart?

Universal Cart is a shopping cart in Google Search, AI Mode and the Gemini app that holds products from many retailers at once. It launched in the US on 15 October 2026. Google monitors items for deals and price drops, and shoppers can check out on Google or continue on the retailer’s site.

Who is the merchant of record with Universal Cart?

The retailer. Whether the shopper pays on Google with Google Pay or finishes on the retailer’s website, the brand remains the merchant of record and fulfils the order.

How do merchants join Universal Cart?

US merchants enable capabilities in the UCP integration hub in Merchant Center: cart transfer to their own site, native checkout on Google, and identity linking. The hub is rolling out gradually, with access through a waitlist and early access.

Is Universal Cart available outside the US?

Not yet. Google says Canada and Australia are next, from next year. No date has been announced for the UK, Europe or the GCC, but the same product feed data already powers Shopping ads and AI Mode results there.
