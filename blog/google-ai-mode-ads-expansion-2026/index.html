Google Ads · AI Mode

Your Search Ads Can Now Appear Inside AI Answers in 10 More Countries.
You Can’t Opt Out

On 28 October Google took ads in AI Mode to Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore, with the UK, Brazil and Mexico due before the year ends. In the US it launched multiturn ads, which appear when people ask follow-up questions. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are eligible automatically. What that means for your account, and for advertisers in Oman and the GCC who are not on the list yet.

Updated Nov 2, 2026Announced 28 October 2026
November 2, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

For two years advertisers asked whether AI search would leave room for ads. Google’s answer, delivered on its earnings call on 28 October, is that the ads come with the answers. In thirteen more countries this year, an AI Mode response can include a labelled text or Shopping ad drawn from campaigns advertisers already run. There is no separate campaign to set up and no switch to turn it off.

+10countries with AI Mode ads from 28 October, plus the UK, Brazil and Mexico later in Q4
3×longer queries in AI Mode than in traditional search, on average in the US
+40%average monthly growth in follow-up queries in AI Mode in the US
In short
  • No opt-out: Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns running in those countries are eligible for AI Mode placements.
  • Multiturn ads launched in the US: ads can appear on the answer to a follow-up question, not just the first one.
  • Text and Shopping ads appear today; Google is experimenting with local inventory and vehicle ads. Pakistan gets text ads only.
  • Oman and the GCC are not on the announced list. Use the time to prepare feeds, landing pages and broad match.

What changes in the auction

A traditional search is a few words. An AI Mode question is a paragraph, and the follow-ups narrow it further: “which of these is quieter”, “which can be delivered by Friday”. Google matches ads to that whole conversation. Exact-match keyword lists matter less; your product data, landing pages and the breadth of your matching matter more.

1

Feeds become ad copy

Shopping ads in answers draw on your product titles, attributes and prices. Missing details such as size, noise level or material mean you cannot match the question.

2

Broad match and AI Max carry the reach

Long questions rarely match a short keyword. Campaigns that use broad match or AI Max are the ones most likely to enter these answers.

3

Landing pages must answer the follow-up

Someone who asked about noise levels lands on your page wanting that number. Put specifications and comparisons where they can find them.

4

Reporting is still thin

AI Mode traffic is reported inside your normal campaigns. Watch search terms and conversion rates for changes rather than waiting for a separate report.

For advertisers in Oman and the GCC

Google has not announced AI Mode ads for the GCC. That gives you a window. When ads do arrive, accounts with rich feeds, broad matching and pages that answer real questions will be eligible from the first day; accounts built on short exact-match keyword lists will not.

  • Rewrite top product titles to include the attributes people ask about.
  • Test broad match or AI Max on one campaign now, so you have results before the auction changes.
  • Add an FAQ block to your best-selling pages in Arabic and English.

The keyword was a guess at a question. Now the question arrives in full.

Common questions

Which countries have ads in Google AI Mode?

Ads in AI Mode run in the United States. On 28 October 2026 Google began expanding them to Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore, and said the UK, Brazil and Mexico would follow in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Can advertisers opt out of AI Mode ads?

No. In the markets where AI Mode ads run, Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are eligible for AI Mode placements without a separate opt-in or opt-out setting.

What are multiturn ads?

Multiturn ads appear on AI Mode’s answers to follow-up questions, not only on the first question. Google launched them in the United States on 28 October 2026.

Are AI Mode ads available in Oman?

Not as of early November 2026. Oman and the other GCC countries were not on the list Google announced on 28 October 2026.
