Google Is Moving Hotel Ads Into Search Campaigns This Quarter.
What Hotels in Oman Should Do Before It Happens

Google is migrating Hotel campaigns into new Search campaigns for Travel in the fourth quarter of 2026, after moving Travel campaigns earlier this year. The new campaigns bring hotel feeds and formats into standard Search, powered by AI Max, and copy the closest matching settings from your old campaigns. You can switch by hand before the automatic move. What changes, what to check, and a migration checklist for hotels and resorts.

Updated Nov 23, 2026Hotel migration reported 18 November 2026
November 23, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

For hotels, Google’s hotel ads have been a separate world: price feeds, a campaign type of their own and placements in Google Hotels and Maps. Google is folding that into Search. Search campaigns for Travel, announced in April, combine travel feeds with ordinary search ads and use AI Max to match searches and write ads. Travel campaigns moved first; Hotel campaigns are next, this quarter.

In short
  • Hotel campaigns become Search campaigns for Travel; settings are copied as closely as possible.
  • The new campaigns use AI Max, so matching and ad text become more automated.
  • Moving manually first lets you check settings before the busy winter weeks.
  • Independent hotels should watch brand searches closely; some hoteliers doubt the benefit for single properties.

Common questions

Are Google Hotel campaigns going away?

Google is migrating Hotel campaigns to new Search campaigns for Travel in the fourth quarter of 2026, according to trade reports. The new campaigns copy the closest matching settings.

What are Search campaigns for Travel?

Search campaigns that bring travel feeds and formats into standard Search, powered by AI Max. Google announced them in April 2026.

Can I switch before Google does it automatically?

Yes. Reports say advertisers can transition manually before the automatic migration.

What should an Oman hotel check first?

Your rates and availability feed, booking conversion tracking, brand campaign coverage, and the automated ad text in each language.
