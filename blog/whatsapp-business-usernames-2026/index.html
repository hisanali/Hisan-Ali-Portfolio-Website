WhatsApp Usernames Are Coming.
Reserve Your Business Name Before Someone Else Does

WhatsApp is adding usernames, so people can message a business by name instead of a phone number. Businesses have been able to reserve one since 29 June in WhatsApp Manager or Meta Business Suite, and names go live country by country. For businesses using the WhatsApp API, customers who use a username may arrive with a business-scoped user ID instead of a phone number, which affects CRMs and automations. What to do, and a readiness check.

Updated Nov 12, 2026Reservations open since 29 June 2026
November 12, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

In Oman, a business’s WhatsApp number is often its real shopfront: printed on signs, cars, bags and Instagram bios. Usernames change that. A customer will be able to search for @yourbrand and message you, without saving a number first. That makes your name more valuable, and it raises the risk of someone else taking a name close to yours.

In short
  • Reserve your username in WhatsApp Manager or Meta Business Suite; it stays “reserved” until usernames launch in your country.
  • Aim to match your Instagram and Facebook handles so customers recognise you.
  • If you use the API, prepare for chats identified by a business-scoped user ID instead of a phone number.
  • Exact launch dates for Oman haven’t been published; check your WhatsApp Manager status.

What to do now

1

Reserve the name

Use your brand name, the same as on Instagram if you can. Avoid numbers and underscores that people won’t remember.

2

Plan the switch on print

When usernames go live in Oman, update signs, packaging and bios to show @yourbrand next to your number.

3

Check your CRM and automations

If your tools key customers by phone number, ask your provider how they will handle business-scoped IDs.

4

Keep consent records

Common questions

Can businesses get a WhatsApp username?

Yes. Businesses have been able to reserve usernames since 29 June 2026 through WhatsApp Manager, Meta Business Suite or API partners. Reserved names go live when usernames launch in each region.

When will WhatsApp usernames launch in Oman?

Meta has rolled usernames out country by country and has not published a date for Oman in the sources we checked. Your WhatsApp Manager shows whether your username is reserved or approved.

What is a WhatsApp BSUID?

A business-scoped user ID that identifies a customer to a specific business on the WhatsApp API when the customer has not shared a phone number, for example after messaging by username.

Should my username match my Instagram handle?

Ideally yes, so customers recognise you across apps. Reserve it early in case someone else claims a similar name.
