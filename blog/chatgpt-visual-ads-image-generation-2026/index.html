ChatGPT Ads · New Format

ChatGPT Will Show Ads While It Draws Your Picture.
What Brands Should Know About Visual Ads

OpenAI is testing image-led ads that appear while ChatGPT generates an image: ask for a birthday card or a logo sketch and a labelled brand image may sit beside it. The test is US-only, with a first group of advertisers, on Free and Go plans. How the format works, what is still unknown, and how to think about prompts as ad targeting.

Updated Oct 31, 2026Announced 5 October 2026US test
October 31, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

Image generation is one of ChatGPT’s most popular uses, and it comes with a pause: a few seconds while the picture appears. On 5 October OpenAI said it would test ads in that moment. A request for a birthday card, a room makeover or a logo sketch can return next to a clearly labelled image paid for by a brand. It is the first ChatGPT ad format built around pictures rather than answers.

In short
  • Visual ads appear alongside images ChatGPT generates, labelled and separate from the user’s image.
  • OpenAI says ads do not change ChatGPT’s answers or the image it makes.
  • Pricing is unpublished. ChatGPT’s ad system generally sells by impressions, clicks or conversions; whether visual ads follow it is not confirmed.
  • For brands outside the US, this is a preview. Prepare creative and prompt maps now; buy when it reaches your market.

Checked 31 October 2026. Details come from OpenAI’s announcement as reported by TechCrunch and Search Engine Journal, plus guides that describe the wider ChatGPT ads system. We could not confirm that the test has started serving.

Prompts are the new keywords

Search ads match what people type into a search box. Visual ads will presumably match what people ask an AI to draw. Image prompts are rich in intent: a nursery makeover, a wedding invitation, a logo for a new café. Each describes a project someone is about to spend money on.

1

Map the projects, not the products

List the things your customers create before they buy: invitations before a wedding, mood boards before a renovation, menus before a restaurant opens.

2

Make creative that sits beside AI art

Your image will appear next to a freshly generated picture. Clean, real product photography in a matching style will look more useful than a banner.

3

Offer the next step

The user has an idea on screen. The best ad turns it into something real: print this card, book this florist, order these tiles.

4

Expect brand-safety questions

Image prompts can be personal or odd. Ask what exclusions and category controls exist before you buy.

Reasons for caution

  • It is a test. Formats in tests change, and early results come from advertisers, not OpenAI.
  • The evidence is thin. WeightWatchers reported a 15.3% lower cost per acquisition on ChatGPT ads than its blended paid search, measured by DV Rockerbox. That is one brand, on ChatGPT ads in general, not visual ads.
  • Matching is unclear. OpenAI has not explained how an image request is matched to an ad.

People tell an image model exactly what they are about to make. That is the most honest intent signal in advertising.

Common questions

What are ChatGPT visual ads?

Visual ads are an OpenAI test, announced on 5 October 2026, of image-led ads that appear alongside the images ChatGPT generates for users. They are labelled and kept separate from the user’s image.

Who sees ads in ChatGPT image generation?

The test runs in the United States for users on the Free and Go plans. Reports say ads are not shown on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu accounts, or to users identified as under 18.

How much do ChatGPT visual ads cost?

OpenAI has not published pricing for visual ads. Guides describe ChatGPT ads generally as bought by impressions, clicks or conversions, but whether visual ads use the same options is not confirmed.

Can businesses in Oman buy ChatGPT visual ads?

Not yet. The test is limited to the United States and an initial group of advertisers. Businesses elsewhere can prepare creative and map relevant image requests ahead of any wider launch.
