LinkedIn Ads · October Changes

LinkedIn Just Changed Four Things About Its Ads.
Here’s the October Rundown

Pre-roll ads on creators’ videos, with creators paid. Reserved Ads opened to audiences of 100,000. A daily spend allowance moving to twice your daily budget. New follow and profile-click metrics in the API. Each is small on its own; together they change how B2B budgets behave. What changed, what is reported versus confirmed, and a picker that matches your objective and budget to the right LinkedIn format.

01In-Stream Ads: pre-roll comes to LinkedIn

On 15 October LinkedIn began testing In-Stream Ads: when a pre-roll ad runs before an eligible creator’s video, the creator earns. It is invite-only, US-only and limited to select advertisers for now, with broader access “planned over time”. LinkedIn has not published payout shares, prices, eligibility thresholds or brand-safety controls.12

LinkedIn already spends unevenly across a month to hit your average. If the allowance moves to 2×, a single strong day can take double your daily figure, balanced by lighter days. Monthly totals stay inside the budget; daily volatility grows. For campaigns with hard daily caps (a webinar on Thursday, a launch on the 1st), use lifetime budgets and schedules rather than daily figures.3

03New metrics for Thought Leader Ads

Version 202610 of the Marketing API adds two optional metrics to ad analytics: memberFollows and memberProfileClicks. They exist only for Sponsored Updates and member-follower display ads such as Thought Leader Ads. If you promote an executive’s posts, you can now report follows and profile visits the ad produced, not just engagement.4

04What to do

1

Move dated campaigns to lifetime budgets

If a day matters, cap it with a schedule and a lifetime budget, not a daily figure that can double.

2

Ask about In-Stream now

Even if you are outside the US, ask your rep about timing, exclusions and creator categories, so you are ready when it opens.

3

Re-size audiences for Reserved Ads

If you have been building 300,000-member audiences to qualify, you may now be able to reserve tighter, better ones.

4

Report follows on executive ads

Add member follows and profile clicks to your Thought Leader Ads dashboard. They are the point of the format.

LinkedIn is becoming a video platform with a creator economy. B2B budgets will follow the attention.

Common questions

What are LinkedIn In-Stream Ads?

In-Stream Ads are pre-roll video ads that run before eligible creators’ videos on LinkedIn, with the creator earning a share. LinkedIn began testing them on 15 October 2026 with select US creators and advertisers, by invitation. Payout shares, prices and brand-safety controls have not been published.

Did LinkedIn change its daily budget pacing?

An October 2026 recap reports that LinkedIn’s daily spend allowance is moving from 1.5× to 2× the daily budget, with configured budgets and schedules unchanged. LinkedIn has not published a separate announcement, so confirm the current behaviour in Campaign Manager and use lifetime budgets where daily caps matter.

What is the minimum audience for LinkedIn Reserved Ads?

As reported in October 2026, Reserved Ads now accept audiences of 100,000 members, while Premiere video still requires a managed account and a $50,000 minimum reservation budget.

What new metrics did LinkedIn add for Thought Leader Ads?

Version 202610 of the Marketing API adds memberFollows and memberProfileClicks to ad analytics, available for Sponsored Updates and member-follower display ads such as Thought Leader Ads.
