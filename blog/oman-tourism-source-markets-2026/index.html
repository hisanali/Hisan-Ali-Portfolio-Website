European Hotel Guests in Oman Fell 31% This Year.
Where to Point Your Winter Marketing Instead

Hotel revenue at Oman’s three- to five-star hotels fell 11.7% to RO 171.1 million in the first nine months of 2026. European guests, the second-largest group, dropped 30.7% to about 289,000, and GCC guests fell 19.9%, while Asian guests rose 2.9% and Omani guests 3.4%. Total arrivals were almost flat, but Skift reports shorter stays and later bookings. Which markets to target this winter, and a planner that splits your budget by source market.

Updated Nov 15, 2026NCSI data to end of September 2026
November 15, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Oman’s tourism story this year depends on where you look. Arrivals at the border barely moved, down 0.8% to about 2.5 million in the first eight months. Hotels had a much harder time: fewer Europeans, fewer Gulf visitors, shorter stays and later bookings, which Skift links to regional disruption. For anyone marketing hotels, tours or experiences, that means rebalancing who you target.

In short
  • Hotel revenue fell 11.7% to RO 171.1 million in January to September.
  • September occupancy was 47.3%, down 6.4 points, according to CoStar.
  • Omani and Asian guests grew; Europe and the GCC fell sharply.
  • Expect later bookings: keep campaigns running closer to travel dates.

Where to point your marketing

1

Residents first

Omani guests grew. Weekend packages, holidays and family offers in Arabic and English are the steadiest bet.

2

India and Asia

Asian guests rose. Short flights from India make long weekends easy to sell; work with travel agents there.

3

Europe: fewer, but still big

Europeans are still the second-largest group. Reassure on safety, offer flexible cancellation and work through tour operators.

4

Book later, market later

Shorter booking windows mean last-minute campaigns matter. Keep budget for the final two weeks before each holiday.

Common questions

How did Oman’s hotels perform to September 2026?

Revenue at three- to five-star hotels fell 11.7% to RO 171.1 million in January to September 2026, according to preliminary NCSI data.

Which visitor markets are falling and which are growing?

European hotel guests fell 30.7% to about 289,200 and GCC guests fell 19.9%, while Asian guests rose 2.9% and Omani guests 3.4%.

Are fewer people visiting Oman?

Total arrivals were almost flat, down 0.8% to about 2.5 million in January to August, but hotels saw fewer guests, shorter stays and later bookings.

Who should Oman tourism businesses target this winter?

Residents and Asian markets are growing, while Europe remains a large market that needs reassurance and flexible terms. Keep budget for last-minute bookings.
