Google Says Ads Are Coming to Its AI Agents.
How to Be the Sponsored Option When an Agent Shops

On its third-quarter call, Google said sponsored options will appear beside the recommendations its AI agents make, much as ads sit beside search and shopping results. Its Gemini agent is due to reach consumers, starting in the Gemini app, and agentic features are heading to Chrome, Android and Workspace. Google still says the Gemini app itself has no ads. What is confirmed, what isn’t, and a readiness check.

Updated Nov 4, 2026Q3 call 28 October 2026
November 4, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

The fear about AI agents is that they will pick one answer and leave no room for anyone else. Google’s view, from chief business officer Philipp Schindler, is that there is almost never a single perfect solution to a shopping decision, so people still want options, and some of those options can be sponsored. That is the plan: an agent researches, compares and recommends, and paid offers appear alongside.

In short
  • Google says sponsored options will appear beside agent recommendations.
  • It has not said when, where or how they will be bought.
  • Ads already run in AI Mode in more countries, so that is the place to prepare now.
  • Agents read product data, prices, stock and reviews; clean feeds come first.

Common questions

Will Google show ads in AI agents?

Yes. On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, Google said sponsored options will appear alongside AI agents’ recommendations. It has not given a launch date or ad format.

Are there ads in the Gemini app?

Google has said there are no ads in the Gemini app. The announced agent ads are a separate plan whose surfaces have not been detailed.

How can a store prepare for AI shopping agents?

Keep a complete product feed with accurate prices, stock, delivery and returns, collect reviews, write descriptive titles and track purchases with values.

Where can I buy AI ads from Google today?

Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode run through Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, in the countries where Google has launched them.
