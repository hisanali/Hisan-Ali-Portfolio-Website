Instagram’s Originality Rule:
Why Reposting Brands Are Disappearing From Discovery

Instagram now stops recommending accounts that mostly post content they did not make, including photos and carousels. What counts as original, how to check your account, and the new October tools that reward brands doing it right.

Key takeaways
  • Accounts that mostly post content they did not create, or did not meaningfully change, are no longer recommended to people who do not follow them.
  • The rule now covers photos and carousels, not just Reels. Followers still see your posts.

Scroll through Instagram accounts for Omani shops and service businesses and a pattern appears: supplier photos, screenshots of customer messages, the same viral Reel three other shops posted that week, a quote card found on Pinterest. That used to be harmless filler. In 2026, it can quietly remove your account from Explore and Reels recommendations.

01What the rule says

On 30 April 2026, Instagram head Adam Mosseri announced that accounts mainly sharing content they did not create, or did not meaningfully transform, would lose eligibility to be recommended to people who do not follow them. A rule that had applied to Reels was extended to photos and carousels.12

Two details matter. First, followers are not affected: your existing audience still sees reposts. The cost is growth, because new people find accounts mainly through recommendations. Second, Instagram’s Account Status page in Settings shows whether your account is currently eligible to be recommended.3

02What counts as original

Not original

Supplier or stock photos as they are, other accounts’ Reels re-uploaded, memes and quote cards, screenshots, someone else’s video with your logo or a border added.

Grey area

Customer photos with permission, a trend audio over your own footage, a carousel mixing your photos with sourced ones. Make your contribution obvious.

Original

Your own photos and videos, your team on camera, behind the scenes, how-to content, your commentary or reaction that adds something new to someone else’s clip.

Mosseri’s line is “meaningfully transform”. A reaction with real commentary, a remake of a trend using your product or a stitched explanation adds something. A watermark does not.2

03The Oman brand problem

Retail

Supplier photos

Many shops in Oman post the same manufacturer photos as every other reseller. Shoot your own: a phone, daylight and your shop in the background are enough.

Services

Screenshot testimonials

WhatsApp screenshots of happy customers are not original content. A 20-second video of the customer, or of you explaining the result, is.

Restaurants

Reposted creator videos

Share creators’ visits through Instagram’s repost or collaboration features instead of re-uploading them to your grid.

All

Trend chasing

Re-uploading a trending Reel is now risky. Remake it with your product and your people. See trendjacking for Oman brands.

Feed originality audit

How original is your feed?

Count your grid posts from the last 30 days (Reels, photos and carousels; not Stories) and sort them into three groups.

Runs in your browser; nothing is saved. Instagram has not published an exact threshold; “mostly” is treated here as more than half, with 70% as a comfortable target. Check Account Status in the app for your real status.

Common questions

Does Instagram penalise reposting in 2026?

Since 30 April 2026, accounts that mostly post content they did not create, or did not meaningfully transform, are no longer recommended to people who do not follow them. This now applies to photos and carousels as well as Reels. Followers still see the posts.

Does adding my logo make a repost original?

No. Borders, watermarks or captions added to someone else’s content are not treated as meaningful transformation. Commentary, reactions or remakes with your own footage are.

How do I know if my account is affected?

Open Settings and check Account Status, which shows whether your account is eligible to be recommended. Then review your last 30 days of posts and shift towards original content.

Can I still share customer and creator content?

Yes. Use Instagram’s repost, Story share and collaboration features, or add tagged posts to your grid, rather than downloading and re-uploading the content as your own post.
