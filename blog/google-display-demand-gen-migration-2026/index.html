Google Is Moving Display Campaigns Into Demand Gen.
Why Advertisers in Oman Should Move Before Google Does

Standalone Display campaigns are being folded into Demand Gen, and Google will migrate the ones you leave behind. The Display Network stays, but Manual CPC, campaign-level exclusions and lift studies on Display do not carry over as they are. What changes, and a planner for moving on your own terms.

Updated Nov 9, 2026Migration tool live since June 2026
November 9, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Google is retiring standalone Display campaigns. The Display Network itself is not going anywhere: it moves inside Demand Gen, next to YouTube, Discover and Gmail. Google announced the change in May, a migration tool started appearing in accounts in June, and any eligible campaigns you have not moved will eventually be migrated automatically. Many Oman accounts still run Display remarketing or cheap awareness campaigns, so it is worth moving them on your own terms.

In short
  • Display campaigns move into Demand Gen; the Display Network stays available inside it.
  • Migrated campaigns start on Display only through channel controls, with up to 42 days of history.
  • Manual CPC, campaign-level content exclusions and Brand Lift or Search Lift on Display do not carry over as they are.
  • Google will migrate remaining campaigns automatically. The cut-off for creating new Display campaigns has no confirmed date.

How to move on your own terms

1

List what you run

Note each Display campaign, its bidding, audiences, exclusions and last 30 days of results, so you can compare after the move.

2

Replace Manual CPC

Set a Target CPC close to your current average cost per click, then adjust after two weeks of data.

3

Move exclusions up

Rebuild campaign-level placement and content exclusions at account level before you migrate, so ads do not appear where you blocked them.

4

Start on Display, then test

Keep the migrated campaign on Display only at first. Once it is stable, test adding YouTube and Discover in a separate campaign.

Common questions

Is the Google Display Network going away?

No. Standalone Display campaigns are being retired, but the Display Network is available inside Demand Gen, and you can run Demand Gen on Display only using channel controls.

What happens if I do not migrate my Display campaigns?

Google says eligible campaigns that are not moved manually will eventually be migrated automatically.

What does not carry over to Demand Gen?

Manual CPC bidding is not available (Target CPC replaces it), campaign-level content exclusions move to account level, and Brand Lift and Search Lift are not supported for the Display Network in Demand Gen.

When can I no longer create Display campaigns?

Google has not confirmed a date. Some industry reports point to early 2027, so plan your move now rather than waiting.
