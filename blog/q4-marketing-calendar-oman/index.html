Seasonal Strategy · Planning

Oman’s Q4 Is
Four Seasons, Not One

National Day and White Friday land one week apart this year. Give each moment its own job.

September 24, 20266 min read + plannerBy Hisan Ali

11 November: 11.11.
20 November: National Day.
27 November: White Friday.

When moments crowd together, the plan’s job is to stop them competing with each other, not to shout louder in all of them.

Picture a Muscat perfume brand planning the last quarter. Last year it ran one long “Q4 sale” from early November to the end of December. Sales rose, but margin fell, and customers learned to wait for the next code. Nobody could say which week actually worked.

This year the calendar is even tighter. National Day falls on 20 November, and White Friday, the regional name many retailers use for Black Friday, lands on 27 November, exactly one week later. The official National Day days off have been moved to Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26 November, so White Friday now sits inside a four-day break. [1] [2] Those are two very different moments competing for the same customers, budget and team.

This is a fictional teaching example. The dates are real; the brand and figures are not.

The four moments and what makes each different

Q4 2026 in Oman. Holiday arrangements are set by official announcement.
MomentDateWhat customers want
11.11Wednesday 11 NovemberMarketplace deals, especially electronics, fashion and beauty bought online.
National Day20 November (days off 25–26 November)Pride, gatherings, gifts and time off. Emotion and occasion matter more than discount depth.
White FridayFriday 27 NovemberPrice. Shoppers compare deals and expect clear, genuine discounts.
Year-endDecember, to 31 DecemberGifts, cooler-weather outings, visitors in town and year-end budgets for business buyers.

One detail matters this year: 20 and 21 November 2026 fall on a Friday and Saturday, Oman’s weekend, so the government has moved the National Day holiday to Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26 November. [2] Together with the weekend, that creates a four-day break from 25 to 28 November with White Friday in the middle. Expect people to travel or stay home and shop online. Confirm delivery promises, opening hours and staff rotas for those four days, and plan whether your White Friday offer should start on 25 November. The National Day dates themselves are set by Royal Decree 15/2025. [1]

Then the quarter rolls straight into the next big planning window: Ramadan 2027, currently expected around 8 February. The Ramadan 2027 runway starts before December ends.

Give every moment one job

The biggest Q4 mistake is running the same discount message through every moment. Customers stop listening, and your National Day content ends up sounding like a clearance sale. Instead, give each moment a single job:

11.11

Test

Use it to test offers and creatives on a smaller budget. Learn which products and messages move, then carry the winners into White Friday.

National Day

Connect

Lead with meaning, not markdowns: limited editions, gifting, stories of local craft and service. Treat national symbols with respect.

White Friday

Convert

This is the price moment. Make offers clear, genuine and protected by margin rules. Keep it short so it stays special.

Year-end

Keep

Turn Q4 buyers into repeat customers: thank-you messages, gift-card reminders and early access to Ramadan collections.

Separating the jobs also separates the measurement. When each moment has one goal, you can finally tell which one earned its budget. If you are tempted to discount across all four, run the numbers first with the discount profit test.

Pace the budget instead of spreading it

With four moments in seven weeks, an even daily budget spends too much in the quiet days and too little at the peaks. A simple pacing rule helps:

A STARTING SPLIT · ADJUST TO YOUR BUSINESS
Warm-up
About 20% from mid-October: build audiences with useful content, product stories and National Day creative.
11.11
About 10%: a contained test of offers and creative.
National Day
About 25%: occasion-led content, gifting and creators.
White Friday
About 30%: short, focused conversion campaigns to warmed audiences.
Year-end
About 15%: retention, gifting and early Ramadan sign-ups.

These shares are a starting point, not a benchmark. A gifting business may weight National Day and year-end higher; an electronics retailer may weight 11.11 and White Friday higher. What matters is deciding deliberately, and writing down what each slice is meant to achieve. Advertising costs often rise during peak weeks, so audiences warmed up earlier are worth more than cold traffic bought at the peak.

The six-week runway for each moment

  1. Six weeks out: plan and book. Confirm the offer, stock, margin rules and delivery capacity. Book creators and photography. For creators, check the promotion licence first; see buy outcomes, not followers.
  2. Four weeks out: produce and test. Make creative in Arabic and English. Test the landing pages on a phone; a slow page wastes peak traffic, as the Core Web Vitals guide explains.
  3. Two weeks out: warm up. Tease the collection, grow your WhatsApp and email lists, and let customers save or wish-list products.
  4. Launch week: go live and watch. Check stock, reply times and checkout daily. Pause anything that breaks a margin rule.
  5. The week after: review. Record what each moment achieved against its one job before the next one starts.

Q4 readiness check

After December: the quarter that pays for itself

The quarter’s real return often arrives later. Customers who first bought in November can be the easiest Ramadan customers in February, if you ask permission to stay in touch and give them a reason to come back. Measure Q4 on what it adds to the next six months, not just the last six weeks.

Questions about Q4 planning

Should we run a discount on National Day?

You can, but it is rarely the strongest message. National Day rewards meaning: gifts, limited editions and pride in local products. Keep the deep discounts for White Friday, when shoppers expect them.

We are a small business. Do we need all four moments?

No. Choose the one or two that fit your customers and do them well. A focused National Day campaign beats a stretched presence in all four.

When will the National Day holiday be observed in 2026?

National Day is on 20 November, but because 20 and 21 November fall on Oman’s weekend this year, the public holiday is observed on Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26 November 2026. With the weekend, that makes a four-day break from 25 to 28 November, including White Friday on 27 November.

References & method

Sources checked September 24, 2026. The perfume brand, budget shares and figures are illustrative. Retail events such as 11.11 and White Friday are commercial dates, not public holidays.
