Oman’s Central Bank Says Mobile Payments Now Lead.
Is Your Checkout Keeping Up?

The Central Bank of Oman’s Financial Stability Report 2026 says digital channels are replacing cash withdrawals and cheques, with mobile payments leading and card payments rising. In September the bank launched a National Fintech Strategy and a single portal for fintech firms, and in November it signed a cooperation memorandum on digital currencies with China’s central bank. In 2025, card terminal payments passed RO 7.5 billion and QR transactions more than doubled. What this means for how your customers pay, and a checkout check.

Updated Nov 15, 2026Financial Stability Report, November 2026
November 15, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Ask a shop owner in Muscat how customers pay and the answer has changed fast: fewer notes, more cards, a lot more phones. The central bank’s numbers confirm it. Fee caps on QR payments and free local transfers for individuals and SMEs have made digital payment cheaper for both sides, and new rules for buy now, pay later make instalments safer to offer.

Common questions

How fast are digital payments growing in Oman?

Central Bank of Oman data shows card terminal payments passed RO 7.5 billion in 2025, up 33.2%, and QR code transactions rose 133.5%. Its Financial Stability Report 2026 says mobile payments now lead.

What is Oman’s National Fintech Strategy?

A strategy launched on 22 September 2026 by the Central Bank of Oman with the Financial Services Authority and the Estidama programme, together with a single portal for fintech firms. It focuses on financial inclusion, digital infrastructure, startups and regulation; no numerical targets were published.

Which payment methods should a shop in Oman offer?

QR, card and instant bank transfer, with cash on delivery as a fallback. Buy now, pay later can help for higher-value items.

How can I reduce cash-on-delivery refusals?

Encourage prepayment with a small incentive, confirm orders by WhatsApp before dispatch and make digital payment the default at checkout.
