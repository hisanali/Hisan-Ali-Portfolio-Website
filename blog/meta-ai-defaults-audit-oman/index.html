Meta’s AI Is Editing Your Ads:
The October 2026 Settings Audit

Video made from your photos, product browsing added to running ads, and an assistant that can change campaigns. What Meta switched on in October 2026, what is still a test, and the ten-minute audit every Oman advertiser should run.

Key takeaways
  • Meta can now turn static images into video automatically through Advantage+ creative.
  • Product browsing is reported to switch on in ads that are already running from 28 October 2026, unless you opt out.
  • An AI assistant that plans and changes campaigns is in testing, with a preview and approval step.
  • None of this is bad. But every automatic change needs a human check for Arabic text, prices and cultural fit.

Meta’s pitch is simple: give the AI your product, budget and goal, and let it handle the rest. In October 2026 it moved several steps closer to that. Some features arrive as options; others are switched on by default, including in ads you approved weeks ago. For a business in Oman, where an automatically cropped photo or a machine-made Arabic caption can go wrong in public, the details matter.

This follows a pattern. In September 2025, Meta made dynamic media the default for catalog ads, adding product videos automatically.4 Each new default is small. Together, they mean the ad people see can differ quite a lot from the ad you approved.

02Why it matters in Oman

Language

Arabic text and captions

Auto-generated text and video may handle Arabic poorly: broken letter joins, wrong direction or awkward phrasing. Customers notice.

Culture

Imagery and context

Cropping, motion effects and generated backgrounds can produce images that do not suit local norms, especially during Ramadan.

Prices

Accuracy and the law

Product browsing pulls prices from your catalogue. If it is out of date, ads show the wrong price. See promotion rules.

Measurement

What changed the result?

When Meta changes the ad mid-flight, it is harder to know whether results moved because of you or the AI. Note every change.

Settings audit

The ten-minute Meta audit

Work through each check in Ads Manager. Tick it when done; the list shows what to do next.

Runs in your browser; nothing is saved. Menu names in Ads Manager change often; look for the closest equivalent.

03What to keep on

The goal is not to switch everything off. Many automatic features improve results, especially for small teams without a designer. A sensible approach:

  • Keep on what you have tested and reviewed: placement optimisation and simple image adjustments often help.
  • Test static-to-video and product browsing on one campaign, comparing cost per sale or lead.
  • Switch off anything that touches text, prices or faces until you have reviewed it.

For the conversation side of Meta’s AI push, see Meta Business Agent, and for creative that works, the Facebook marketing guide for Oman.

Common questions

Is Meta changing my ads automatically?

Some features are on by default. Advantage+ creative enhancements can alter ads, and trade press reports that product browsing will be added to existing ads from 28 October 2026 unless advertisers opt out. Check each ad’s creative settings and your Ads Manager notifications.

How do I turn off Advantage+ creative enhancements?

Open the ad, go to the creative section and find the enhancements settings, then switch off the ones you do not want. Check advanced preview to confirm. Settings can sit at more than one level, so review each active ad.

Should I use static-to-video?

Test it. It can give image-only advertisers video placements cheaply, but review every generated video for quality, Arabic text and cultural fit before scaling.

Can Meta’s AI assistant change my budget?

The assistant that plans and updates campaigns is in testing and is described as showing a preview for approval before changes apply. Decide internally who approves such changes.
