ChatGPT Ads · Buying Guide

There Are Now Five Ways to Buy ChatGPT Ads.
Which Route Fits Your Business

ChatGPT ads have gone from one US pilot to a crowded market of buying routes: OpenAI’s own self-serve Ads Manager, ad-tech partners such as Criteo and StackAdapt, a managed pilot through Amazon DSP, and, since 10 November, AI campaign tools such as Supermetrics that can run ChatGPT Ads alongside Google and Meta. Each route has different reach, controls and minimums. A plain guide to the options, and a picker for a business in Oman or the Gulf.

Updated Nov 15, 2026Updated with routes announced to 10 November 2026
November 15, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

When ChatGPT started showing ads, the only way in was a handful of big US brands working directly with OpenAI. In 2026 that changed quickly. Now the question for a business in Oman is less “can I advertise in ChatGPT?” and more “through whom?”, because the route decides what targeting, reporting and help you get.

In short
  • Self-serve Ads Manager from OpenAI is the simplest route where it is available.
  • Ad-tech partners (Criteo, StackAdapt) add retargeting and links to other channels.
  • Amazon DSP runs a managed US pilot using Amazon’s shopping data.
  • Check country availability for each route before planning an Oman campaign.

Choosing a route

1

Start direct if you can

Self-serve gives you the most control over budget and creative. Switch off enhancements you haven’t reviewed.

2

Go through a partner for reach

If you already run programmatic campaigns, a partner can add ChatGPT as one more channel and retarget viewers elsewhere.

3

Measure the same way everywhere

Use the same conversion tracking and UTM tags across routes so you can compare them fairly.

4

Answer the question

ChatGPT ads appear beside answers. Ads that help with the question people asked work better than slogans.

Common questions

How can I buy ads on ChatGPT?

Through OpenAI’s self-serve Ads Manager where available, through ad-tech partners such as Criteo and StackAdapt, through Amazon DSP’s US pilot, or through AI campaign tools such as Supermetrics that connect to ChatGPT Ads.

Can I buy ChatGPT ads through Amazon?

Amazon Ads and OpenAI began a pilot in September 2026 that lets select US advertisers buy ChatGPT ads through Amazon DSP as a managed service, on a cost-per-click or CPM basis.

Are ChatGPT ads available in Oman?

Availability varies by route and changes often, and reports differ on the number of countries. Check the platform you plan to use for Oman and your other markets.

What makes a good ChatGPT ad?

One that helps with the question the user asked, with clear facts such as price, availability and delivery, and a landing page that continues the answer.
