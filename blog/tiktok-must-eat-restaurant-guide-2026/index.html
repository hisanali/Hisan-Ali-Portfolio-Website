TikTok Just Built a Restaurant Guide From 40 Million Posts.
You Can’t Pay to Be in It

On 28 October TikTok launched Must Eat, a restaurant guide built from posts, reviews and comments, starting in New York, Los Angeles and the San Francisco Bay Area. Restaurants cannot buy a spot, and some can be booked inside the app. It is US-only for now, but it shows where food discovery is heading, including for cafés and restaurants in Muscat. What Must Eat is, how it ranks places, and what to do before it arrives.

Updated Nov 2, 2026Launched 28 October 2026Three US metro areas
November 2, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

Restaurants have spent years making TikToks in the hope that one would travel. Must Eat turns all those posts into something more durable: a ranked guide inside the app, sortable by neighbourhood, cuisine, price and rating. It draws on roughly 40 million posts, reviews and comments, and TikTok says no restaurant can pay to be featured. The ranking is earned by what people film and say.

In short
  • Must Eat sits in TikTok search, the Local feed, restaurant pages and location links on videos.
  • It splits places into everyday eats and elevated dining, and some restaurants can be booked through OpenTable in the app.
  • No paid placement for now, and independent restaurants and local specialities take priority.
  • It extends TikTok GO, launched earlier in 2026 for hotels, tours and attractions.

Checked 2 November 2026. Must Eat is available only in three US metro areas. TikTok has not announced it for the Middle East.

What gets a place ranked

TikTok has not published its formula. The inputs it describes are posts, reviews and comments, so the practical question is simple: how often are customers filming your food, tagging your location and saying good things underneath?

1

Give people something to film

A signature dish, a pour, a reveal. Places that appear in many customer videos have a dish people want to show, not just eat.

2

Make the location tag easy

Use one consistent name and claim your place on TikTok. Ask customers to tag the location, not just your account.

3

Answer comments

Comments are an input. Replying keeps the thread positive and gives questions an answer future diners can read.

4

Get bookable

Where booking exists, a guide that can book a table beats one that cannot. Have an online booking or WhatsApp booking link ready.

What it means in Oman

Muscat diners already pick cafés and restaurants from TikTok and Instagram before they check Google Maps. When a guide like Must Eat arrives, it will rank what people already post. Restaurants with years of customer videos tagged to the right location will start ahead. Remember too that promoting packaged food products in Oman now needs the food safety centre’s approval, so keep creator captions to the dish and the experience.

You can’t buy the ranking. You can give people a reason to film.

Common questions

What is TikTok Must Eat?

Must Eat is a restaurant guide inside TikTok, launched on 28 October 2026 in New York, Los Angeles and the San Francisco Bay Area. It is built from about 40 million posts, reviews and comments and lets users sort places by neighbourhood, cuisine, price and rating.

Can restaurants pay to appear in TikTok Must Eat?

No. TikTok says restaurants cannot pay to be featured, and independent restaurants and local specialities take priority, at least for now.

Can you book a table through TikTok?

Some restaurants in Must Eat can be booked directly in TikTok through OpenTable.

Is TikTok Must Eat available in Oman?

No. At launch it covers three US metro areas, and TikTok has not announced it for the Middle East.
