TikTok, Google and Meta Will All Make Your Video Ads Now.
Which One to Use, and What They Can’t Do

From 9 November, advertisers outside North America, including the Gulf, can buy BytePlus tokens to generate TikTok Symphony videos with ByteDance’s Seedance models, which make clips of up to 30 seconds. Google’s Gemini Omni video tool in Asset Studio has been generally available since late August, and Meta reportedly made image-to-video generally available in Advantage+ creative in October. What each does, where they fall short for Arabic markets, and a picker for your next campaign.

Updated Nov 15, 2026TikTok token access from 9 November 2026
November 15, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

A year ago, a small business without a video team made do with photos. Now every major ad platform will turn a product image or a short prompt into a video ad, inside the same account where you run campaigns. The results vary a lot, and none of them knows your customers, your dialect or your brand the way you do.

30seconds: TikTok Symphony’s maximum clip length with Seedance
50reference images or clips Symphony can use per video
Englishthe only prompt language Google’s Asset Studio accepts
In short
  • TikTok Symphony: longest clips; from 9 November paid BytePlus tokens are available outside North America. Prices aren’t published.
  • Google Gemini Omni: edit scenes, voiceover, pacing and aspect ratio with prompts, then export to YouTube, Demand Gen and Performance Max.
  • Meta Advantage+ creative: turns still images into short videos inside your ads.
  • All three need human review, especially for Arabic text, prices and claims.

What they can’t do for you

1

Get Arabic right

Generated on-screen Arabic is often wrong or reversed. Add Arabic captions yourself and check them.

2

Keep claims and prices accurate

AI may add words or numbers you didn’t approve. In Oman, promotions and some claims need approval first.

3

Avoid real people

Never let a tool generate a likeness of a real person. Google now suspends advertisers for AI impersonation.

4

Test against real footage

Run the AI version beside a simple phone-shot video. Real often wins on trust.

Common questions

Can advertisers in the Gulf use TikTok Symphony’s Seedance videos?

TikTok said that from 9 November 2026 advertisers outside North America can buy BytePlus tokens to use Seedance models in Symphony, in countries where Ads Manager and BytePlus both operate. Check your own account for availability and pricing.

How long can AI video ads be on TikTok?

With Seedance 2.5, Symphony can generate videos of up to 30 seconds using up to 50 references.

Can Google Ads make video ads with AI?

Yes. Gemini Omni video creation in Asset Studio lets advertisers generate and refine videos with prompts and export them to YouTube, Demand Gen and Performance Max. Prompts are English only.

Are AI-made video ads labelled?

TikTok adds visible AI labels and an invisible watermark to Symphony videos. Google shows AI details in “How this ad was made” and on the ad in some regions. Meta shows AI information labels.
