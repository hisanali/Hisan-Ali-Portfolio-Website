Google Ads Now Shows the Fake Clicks You Didn’t Pay For.
How to Read It

A new Invalid traffic card on the Google Ads Overview page shows two numbers: what Google filtered before billing, and what it credited back after. It began rolling out on 12 November. Here’s how to read it, and what it can’t tell you.

Updated Nov 26, 2026Rolling out from 12 November 2026
November 26, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

“Are my competitors clicking my ads?” is one of the most common questions from business owners in Oman. Google has always filtered invalid clicks and credited some back, but the numbers were buried in billing. The new card puts them on the Overview page, so you can see how much was caught and where.

In short
  • Estimated savings: invalid clicks filtered before billing.
  • Credited cost: invalid activity found later and refunded as credit.
  • Credits don’t change Cost or CPA in the Campaigns table.
  • Google says it doesn’t catch everything.

Common questions

Where is the invalid traffic card in Google Ads?

On the Overview page under Campaigns, labelled “Invalid traffic”. It is rolling out gradually from 12 November 2026, so some accounts may not have it yet.

Do I pay for invalid clicks?

Not for those Google filters before billing. Invalid activity found later is credited back to your account.

Why doesn’t my CPA change after credits?

Credits are applied in billing and don’t change Cost or CPA in the Campaigns table.

Does Google catch all invalid clicks?

No. Google says the card doesn’t guarantee every invalid interaction is identified. Check lead quality in your own records too.
