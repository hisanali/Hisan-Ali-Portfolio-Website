Google Ads · Demand Gen

Google Will Now Raise Your YouTube Ad Bids for a Short Sale,
If You Tell It First

Google’s November Demand Gen update, published on 18 November, added seasonality adjustments to Demand Gen campaigns, which run on YouTube, Gmail, Display and Maps. A seasonality adjustment tells Smart Bidding to expect a jump in conversion rate during a short promotion, so it bids up from the first hour instead of learning slowly. It works with Target CPA and Target ROAS, and some accounts don’t show it yet. For businesses in Oman, it’s useful for a New Year sale, an Eid offer or a two-day launch.

Updated Nov 25, 2026Demand Gen Drop, 18 November 2026
November 25, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Smart Bidding sets bids from recent conversion rates. During a short sale, conversion rates jump, but the system only notices after a few days, by which time the sale is nearly over. Seasonality adjustments fix that for Search, Shopping and Performance Max, and now for Demand Gen too, which matters if YouTube Shorts and in-feed video are part of your plan.

In short
  • Demand Gen campaigns now support seasonality adjustments.
  • Use them for short events with a clear start and end, not whole seasons.
  • Set an expected conversion-rate change, for example +40%.
  • It affects YouTube, Gmail, Display and Maps; reports say not Discover.
  • Check your account: the option isn’t visible everywhere yet.

How it works

Setting up a seasonality adjustment for Demand Gen
SettingWhat to enter
WhereTools, under bid strategies’ advanced controls (menu names vary by account)
ScopeCampaign type Demand Gen, or specific campaigns
DatesExact start and end of the sale, in your account time zone
Conversion-rate changeYour realistic estimate from past sales, such as +30% to +60%
Bid strategiesTarget CPA or Target ROAS

Sources: Google’s November Demand Gen Drop, as reported by Relevant Audience, Shopifreaks and Digital Marketing CEO.123

When to use it, and when not

1

Good fits

A two-day New Year sale, an Eid weekend offer, a product launch day or a flash sale announced to your list.

2

Not for long seasons

Ramadan lasts a month. Smart Bidding adapts on its own; use budgets and creative instead.

3

Be realistic

Overstating the jump overspends. Use last year’s numbers for a similar sale.

4

Tracking first

Adjustments only help if conversions are tracked. A broken tag makes bidding guess.

Common questions

Do seasonality adjustments work with Demand Gen?

Yes, from Google’s November 2026 Demand Gen update, for campaigns using Target CPA or Target ROAS. Some accounts may not show the option yet.

How long should a seasonality adjustment be?

They are designed for short events of a few days, about a week at most. For longer seasons, let Smart Bidding adapt and manage budgets instead.

What number should I enter?

The expected change in conversion rate, based on a similar past sale. If a sale converted at 2.4% against a normal 1.5%, that’s +60%.

Does it affect Discover placements?

Reports on the API beta say the adjustment applies to YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps, not Discover.
