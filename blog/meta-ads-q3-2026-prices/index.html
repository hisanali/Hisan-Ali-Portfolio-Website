Meta Ads Cost 14% More Than a Year Ago:
What Meta’s Q3 2026 Numbers Mean for You

Meta’s third-quarter results, published on 21 October 2026, show ad revenue up 26% to $63.1 billion. Behind that headline: the average price of an ad rose 14%, almost 10 million advertisers used Meta’s AI creative tools, and creator-led ads became a $15 billion business. What each number means for your budget, and a calculator to see what rising prices do to your reach.

Key takeaways
  • The average price per ad rose 14%. The same budget buys roughly 12% fewer impressions than a year ago.
  • Meta says its AI systems are raising conversions, which is part of why prices can rise. Results, not reach, are the fair test.
  • Nearly 10 million advertisers used AI creative tools, and video generation use grew 25% in a quarter.
  • Partnership (creator) ads grew more than 97% to a $15 billion annual run rate.

Meta’s revenue grows two ways: more ads shown and a higher price per ad. This quarter, price did more of the work. Meta’s explanation is that its AI ranking and recommendation systems are delivering more conversions per ad, so advertisers bid more.2 On the call, Meta said a new generative recommendation model in Facebook Feed lifted conversions in the segments where it was tested.

If results rose

Higher prices can be fine

If your cost per purchase or lead held steady while CPM rose, the auction is working for you. Judge by cost per result.

If results fell

You are paying the average

If CPM rose and conversions did not, your ads are not benefiting from the better targeting. Fix creative and tracking first.

04What to do in Q4

1

Report cost per result, not CPM

Rising CPMs are expected. Agree with your team that success is cost per purchase or lead, compared with last year.

2

Refresh creative every two weeks

Fatigue costs more when every impression is pricier. Test AI variations, but review them before they run.

3

Test one creator partnership

It is Meta’s fastest-growing format for a reason. Start with one creator and compare against your best brand ad.

4

Fix tracking before the peak

Check events in Events Manager and turn on the Conversions API. Better signals mean cheaper results.

Meta’s best quarter is not automatically yours. The price went up for everyone; only better ads and better data bring your costs down.

Common questions

Did Meta ad prices go up in 2026?

Yes. In Q3 2026, Meta reported that the average price per ad rose 14% year on year, while ad impressions rose 10%. Advertising revenue grew 26% to $63.1 billion.

Why are Meta ads getting more expensive?

More advertisers are competing, and Meta says its AI systems are delivering more conversions per ad, which raises what advertisers are willing to bid. Q4 holiday demand usually pushes prices higher again.

How many advertisers use Meta’s AI creative tools?

Meta said on its Q3 2026 earnings call that nearly 10 million advertisers used at least one generative AI creative tool in the quarter, and that video generation use grew 25% from the previous quarter.

How should I respond to rising Meta ad costs?

Judge performance by cost per result rather than CPM, refresh creative regularly, improve conversion tracking with the Conversions API, and test formats such as partnership ads that Meta reports perform better.
