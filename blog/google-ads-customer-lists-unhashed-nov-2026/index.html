Google Ads Stops Requiring Hashed Customer Lists on 16 November.
How Oman Businesses Should Prepare Their Data

From 16 November 2026, Google Ads Data Manager no longer needs customer data hashed with SHA-256: it is encrypted by default and matched in a trusted execution environment. For businesses in Oman, where many customer lists are WhatsApp numbers, the bigger win is cleaning those numbers. A formatter is below.

Updated Nov 8, 2026Change starts Nov 16, 2026
November 8, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Google is telling advertisers that from 16 November 2026, Google Ads Data Manager will no longer require customer data to be hashed with SHA-256 before it is uploaded. Data is encrypted by default, then decrypted, normalised and matched inside a trusted execution environment. For many businesses in Oman, whose best customer lists are WhatsApp phone numbers, the change is a good moment to clean those lists properly.

In short
  • From 16 November 2026, Data Manager stops requiring SHA-256 hashing.
  • Customer data is encrypted by default and matched inside a trusted execution environment.
  • It covers CRM connections, cloud storage, file uploads and the Data Manager API, which feed Customer Match and enhanced conversions.
  • Google has not said whether existing hashed uploads change, so note your match rates first.

What it means in Oman

1

Clean your phone numbers

Omani mobile numbers are 8 digits and start with 7 or 9. Upload them in one international format: +968 followed by the 8 digits, with no spaces or leading zeros.

2

Upload only consented customers

Use lists of people who agreed to hear from you, and make sure your privacy notice covers advertising use.

3

Write down your match rates

Before 16 November, note the match rate of each Customer Match list, so you can see whether the change helps.

4

Check custom scripts

If a developer hashes data before it is sent through the API, review Google’s new guidance with them before changing anything.

Common questions

What is changing in Google Ads on 16 November 2026?

Google Ads Data Manager no longer requires customer data to be hashed with SHA-256. Data is encrypted by default and matched inside a trusted execution environment.

Does this affect Customer Match?

Yes. The change covers Data Manager uploads through CRM connections, cloud storage, file uploads and the Data Manager API, which are used for Customer Match and enhanced conversions.

Do I need to change anything?

Not necessarily. Note your current match rates, check any custom hashing scripts, and make sure phone numbers and emails are clean and come from customers who agreed to hear from you.

How should Omani phone numbers be formatted?

In international format: +968 followed by the 8-digit number, with no spaces, dashes or leading zeros.
