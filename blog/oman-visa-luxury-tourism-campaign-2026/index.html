Oman Is Using Visa’s Spending Data to Find High-Spending Tourists.
How Hotels and Tour Operators Can Ride the Campaign

At World Travel Market London on 5 November, the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism announced a joint campaign with Visa and the national travel operator. It is open to Visa cardholders worldwide and aimed at luxury, adventure and marine travellers, with Visa’s data used to find the people most likely to book. Packages are promoted through the Experience Oman app, and a new Oman Tourism Hub in eight languages supports travel partners in 26 countries. What it means for local providers, and a checklist to be bookable when the visitors arrive.

Updated Nov 12, 2026Announced 5 November 2026
November 12, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Oman’s visitor numbers have held up this year while hotel revenue has fallen: guests are coming, but spending less. This campaign goes after the other end of the market. Instead of advertising broadly, it uses what Visa knows about how its cardholders travel and spend to reach people likely to book a high-value trip.

  • The campaign is exclusive to Visa cardholders and targets luxury, adventure and marine travellers.

  • No visitor or spending targets were published.

What local providers can do

1

Get into the packages

Contact Visit Oman, the national travel operator, about listing your experience. Its packages are what the campaign sells.

2

Price for value, not discount

High-spending travellers buy private guides, upgrades and exclusivity. Build a premium version of what you offer.

3

Accept cards everywhere

A Visa campaign brings card-first visitors. Card and contactless payment at every touchpoint is the minimum.

4

Look the part online

Professional photos, English and one more language, and fast replies. These travellers research before they book.

Common questions

What is Oman’s campaign with Visa?

A joint promotional campaign by the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism, Visa and the national travel operator, announced on 5 November 2026 at World Travel Market London. It targets Visa cardholders interested in luxury, adventure and marine tourism.

How does Visa’s data help?

Visa’s analytics identify the traveller segments most likely to be interested in Oman, and the results will guide later phases of the campaign.

What is the Oman Tourism Hub?

A digital programme, available in eight languages and covering 26 countries, that helps local and international travel partners develop and promote Omani tourism products.

How can a local tourism business benefit?

List with the national travel operator, offer a premium version of your experience, accept card payments and keep your online presence professional and multilingual.
