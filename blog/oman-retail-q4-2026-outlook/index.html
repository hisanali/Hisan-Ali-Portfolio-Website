Oman’s Shops Grew 5% While Hotels Shrank.
Where the Money Is This Quarter

Official figures show Oman’s wholesale and retail sector grew 5% in the first half of 2026, to about RO 1.74 billion, even as hotel guests fell 13% and shipping routes were disrupted. Payments got cheaper, more people pay digitally, and the year’s biggest shopping dates are weeks away. A data-led plan for November to January, with the dates that matter.

Updated Nov 2, 2026Data: January to June 2026
November 2, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

The first half of 2026 was not easy for Oman’s economy. Shipping routes through the Gulf were disrupted from late February, visitor numbers stalled and hotels lost guests. Yet shops kept growing. The wholesale and retail sector’s contribution to GDP rose 5% to about RO 1.74 billion, and foreign investment in commercial activity rose 9.7% to about RO 305.3 million. Residents kept spending, and increasingly they paid digitally.

+5%wholesale and retail GDP, first half of 2026, to about RO 1.74 billion
−13%guests at 3- to 5-star hotels over the same period
+150%growth in digital payment transactions in 2025
In short
  • Residents, not tourists, carried retail in the first half. Plan Q4 around local shoppers first.
  • Payments are cheaper: local digital transfers are free and QR fees are capped at 0.50%.
  • Four peaks come in ten weeks: National Day (25–26 November holiday), White Friday (27 November), winter visitors and New Year.
  • Ramadan 2027 is expected around 8 February, so January is also planning month.

Checked 2 November 2026. Retail figures are official data reported by Arab News; hotel and payment figures are from our earlier guides and their sources. Discounts and promotions in Oman need approval.

The dates that decide the quarter

1

National Day: pride, not price

The 25–26 November holiday is about celebration and gifting. Use national colours carefully and follow the rules on national symbols.

2

White Friday: protect the margin

One day after the holiday. Bundles and gifts with purchase protect margin better than deep discounts, and every discount needs approval.

3

December: the visitors and the families

Winter visitors from the UAE and India peak with school holidays. Malls, restaurants and gift shops in Muscat and Salalah see both groups.

4

January: plan Ramadan

With Ramadan expected around 8 February, Ramadan creative, stock and approvals need to be settled in January.

Make paying easy

Digital payment transactions in Oman more than doubled in 2025, and since July local transfers are free for customers. Every peak this quarter is a chance to move customers to QR and instant transfer, which cost you less than cards and cancel less than cash on delivery.

Tourists paused. Residents kept shopping. Plan for them first.

Common questions

How did Oman’s retail sector perform in 2026?

Official data showed the wholesale and retail sector’s GDP grew 5% in the first half of 2026 to about RO 1.74 billion, and foreign investment in commercial activities rose 9.7% to about RO 305.3 million.

When is White Friday 2026 in Oman?

White Friday, the Gulf’s name for Black Friday, falls on Friday 27 November 2026, the day after Oman’s National Day holiday on 25 and 26 November.

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

Planning calendars place the start of Ramadan 2027 around 8 February 2027. The exact date depends on the moon sighting announced in Oman.

Do retail promotions in Oman need approval?

Yes. Oman’s Consumer Protection Law prohibits promotional offers or price discounts without a licence or approval from the competent authority.
