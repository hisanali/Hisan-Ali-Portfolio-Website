Google Shopping · Sale Prices

Sale Running Into December?
Google’s Sale Badge May Drop After 30 Days

Google’s Merchant Center help page on sale price annotations, the struck-through “was” price and sale tag on Shopping ads, now says the sale price duration “should not exceed 30 days” and the base price “should ideally be the sole active price at times”. Google hasn’t said exactly what happens after 30 days, but the message is clear: a sale that never ends looks like your real price. With White Friday sales starting on 25 November and December sales close behind, here’s how to keep the badge, and stay inside Oman’s discount rules too.

Updated Nov 24, 2026Merchant Center help page
November 24, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

The sale price annotation is one of the cheapest ways to win a click on Shopping ads and free listings: shoppers see the old price crossed out next to the new one. Google has always required the old price to be real. The new note adds a time limit to the sale itself, which matters for shops that start a White Friday price on 25 November and simply leave it running.

30days: maximum sale length in Google’s new note
30 of 200days the base price must have been active
5–90%discount range that qualifies for the badge
In short
  • Google now says a sale should not run longer than 30 days.
  • The base price must have been active 30 days in the past 200 (not necessarily in a row).
  • The discount must be more than 5% and less than 90%.
  • Use sale_price_effective_date so sales end on time.
  • In Oman, discounts also need both prices shown and the offer notified.

Plan sales in windows

1

Set an end date

Add sale_price_effective_date to your feed, or set start and end dates in your store app, so the sale price switches off on its own.

2

Return to full price between sales

White Friday to early December, then full price, then a New Year sale. A gap keeps the base price real.

3

Use Promotions for longer offers

Codes and “free gift” offers can run through Merchant Center Promotions without changing the price.

4

Match Oman’s rules

Show the original and discounted price, notify the Consumer Protection Authority before the offer and keep the dates on your tags.

Common questions

What is a sale price annotation on Google?

It’s the struck-through original price and sale label Google can show on Shopping ads and free listings when you submit a sale price that meets its rules.

How long can a sale run on Google Shopping?

Google’s help page now says the sale price duration should not exceed 30 days. It doesn’t say what happens after that, but the badge may stop showing.

How long must the original price be active?

At least 30 days in the past 200 days, which don’t need to be consecutive. For store-specific local inventory, 60 days in the past 200.

What discount qualifies?

More than 5% and less than 90% off the base price.

Do Oman’s discount rules also apply?

Yes. Shops in Oman must show the original and discounted price and notify the Consumer Protection Authority before running offers.
