Oman’s Hotels Lost 11% of Revenue This Year.
Omani Guests Are the One Group Still Growing

Revenue at Oman’s three- to five-star hotels fell 11% to about RO 156.5 million in the first eight months of 2026, and guests fell 10.7% to about 1.35 million. GCC guests dropped 19%. Omani guests went the other way, up 2.7% to 583,702, now 43% of all guests. August was the best month in a while. Who to market to this winter, and a planner that splits your budget by guest type.

Updated Nov 4, 2026NCSI data to end of August 2026
November 4, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

The headline numbers are hard reading for hotels: less revenue, fewer guests and lower occupancy than last year, which Arabian Business linked to regional travel disruption. But the detail holds the plan. Omani residents kept booking, and in August hotel revenue rose to RO 18.23 million from RO 14.13 million in July, with guests up 22%. Staycations, weekend escapes and Khareef trips held the market up.

In short
  • Guests at 3–5 star hotels fell 10.7% to about 1.35 million in January to August.
  • Omani guests rose 2.7% and are now 43.1% of the total.
  • GCC guests fell 19%, a big loss for hotels near the borders and in Muscat.
  • For winter: market weekend and holiday stays to residents first, and keep international campaigns targeted.

Who to market to

1

Residents, at weekends

Thursday-to-Saturday packages, family rooms, pool and brunch passes, and National Day holiday stays. Advertise in Arabic and English inside Oman.

2

Expat families

School holidays in December and winter weekends in the mountains and by the sea. Target by area and interest, with clear prices.

3

GCC visitors, selectively

Demand is down, so target cities with direct flights or short drives and offer flexible cancellation to reduce hesitation.

4

European winter sun

Work through tour operators and Visit Oman’s trade partners rather than broad paid ads.

Common questions

How are Oman’s hotels performing in 2026?

NCSI data shows revenue at three- to five-star hotels fell 11% to about RO 156.5 million in January to August 2026, with guests down 10.7% to about 1.35 million and occupancy at 46.5%.

Which hotel guests are growing in Oman?

Omani guests rose 2.7% to 583,702 in the first eight months of 2026, making up 43.1% of all guests. GCC guests fell 19%.

Did hotel demand recover in August 2026?

August revenue rose to RO 18.23 million from RO 14.13 million in July, and guests rose 22.1% to 198,394.

How should Oman hotels market this winter?

Lead with weekend and holiday packages for residents and expat families, target GCC cities with easy access, and reach European winter travellers through tour operators.
