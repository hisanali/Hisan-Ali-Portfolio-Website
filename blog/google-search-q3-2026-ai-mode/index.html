Google · Earnings Decoded

Google Searches Hit a Five-Year High.
Search Revenue Barely Moved. AI Mode Is Why

Alphabet’s third-quarter results, out on 28 October, show the strongest query growth in five years and Search revenue up 12% to $63.35 billion, yet flat on the previous quarter. On the call, Google blamed “lower AI Mode monetization”. What the numbers say, what Google would not say, and a scenario planner for what a shift to AI answers does to your clicks.

Key takeaways
  • Search growth slowed from 19% in Q1 and 17% in Q2 to 12% in Q3, while queries grew at the fastest rate in five years.
  • Google’s explanation: AI Mode monetises less than classic results, for now. It called AI Mode queries “largely incremental” and early ad tests “promising”.
  • Google gave no AI Mode revenue, CPC or ad-load figure. The gap between usage and money is real but unmeasured from outside.
  • For advertisers: more searches do not mean more clicks. Eligibility for AI surfaces and conversion quality matter more than volume.

02The usage–revenue gap

Two things were true at once in Q3: people searched more than they have in years, and Google made almost no more money from Search than the quarter before. On the call, Philipp Schindler cited lower AI Mode monetisation as a reason Search revenue lagged query growth, while describing AI Mode queries as largely incremental and early advertising experiments as promising.34

Fact

Query growth is real

Google says AI features are driving the fastest query growth in five years. People ask more, and ask longer questions.

Fact

Revenue is not following yet

Search revenue was $63.35B in Q3 against $63.27B in Q2. The extra questions are not yet extra money.

Unknown

How much AI Mode earns

No revenue, CPC or ad-load figure was given. Ads in AI Mode are still described as experiments.

Signal

Google wants it fixed

Tests of ads in AI Mode, including for users who connect Gmail, are widening. Expect more ad formats in AI answers through 2027.

03What it means for advertisers

  • Impressions will look healthy, clicks less so. Search Console’s AI report shows impressions only; judge by clicks and conversions in the main report. See how to read it.
  • Eligibility beats bidding. Ads in AI Mode come from broad match, AI Max and Performance Max campaigns. Exact-match-only accounts are mostly absent.
  • Quality rises as quantity falls. An AI-answer click arrives after a comparison. Fewer, better visits mean landing pages that close, not pages that explain.
  • Google will monetise the gap. The usage is there; the ads will follow. Prepare for AI-surface formats rather than waiting to see if they matter.

04What to do in Q4

1

Split your reporting

Track impressions, clicks and conversions separately, and watch the impression-to-click ratio month by month. A widening gap is AI answers at work.

2

Make your campaigns AI-eligible

Test AI Max for Search or broad match with strong conversion data in one campaign before the February 2027 AI Max migration. See AI Max for Search.

3

Feed Google facts

Prices, availability, policies and reviews on your pages are what AI answers quote. Thin pages get summarised; rich pages get cited.

4

Diversify beyond the search box

Social, affiliates and AI assistants are growing faster than paid search this season. See the holiday forecast.

For twenty years, more searches meant more clicks. Q3 is the first quarter where that stopped being automatic.

Common questions

How much did Google Search revenue grow in Q3 2026?

Google Search & other revenue was $63.35 billion in Q3 2026, up 12% year on year but almost flat against Q2’s $63.27 billion. Growth slowed from 19% in Q1 and 17% in Q2.

Why did Google say Search revenue lagged query growth?

On the Q3 2026 earnings call, Google cited lower AI Mode monetisation: AI Mode queries, described as largely incremental, currently earn less than classic results. Google said early advertising experiments in AI Mode were promising but gave no revenue or CPC figures.

Did YouTube ads grow in Q3 2026?

Yes, YouTube advertising revenue was $11.21 billion, up about 9% year on year, slightly below analysts’ $11.34 billion estimate. Google Network revenue was $7.46 billion, up 1.4%.

What should advertisers do about AI Mode?

Track clicks and conversions rather than impressions, make campaigns eligible for AI surfaces through broad match or AI Max, put quotable facts on landing pages, and diversify demand beyond paid search while AI-answer ad formats mature.
