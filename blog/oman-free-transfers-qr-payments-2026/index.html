Free Transfers and Cheaper QR:
What Oman’s Payment Changes Mean for Your Checkout

Since 1 July, local digital transfers in Oman are free for individuals and SMEs, and the maximum merchant fee on QR “Scan and Pay” has dropped from 0.75% to 0.50%, capped at OMR 2. Digital payments more than doubled last year. For shops, restaurants and Instagram sellers this changes which payment option to push, how to word it, and how much margin a sale keeps. The facts, the opportunity and a savings calculator.

Key takeaways
  • Customers pay nothing to send you money by instant transfer or e-wallet within Oman. The “transfer fee” objection is gone.
  • Merchants pay at most 0.50% on QR Scan and Pay, never more than OMR 2 per payment. On larger orders that is far below typical card fees.
  • QR is still small but fast-growing: transaction numbers rose 133.5% in 2025, and total value nearly tripled to about RO 8 million.
  • The marketing job is to make the cheap option the easy, obvious choice: on the counter, on the product page and inside WhatsApp chats.

02How fast Oman is going cashless

The fee cuts land on a market that is already moving. In 2025 the number of digital payment transactions in Oman rose from about 67 million to more than 168 million. The value of local electronic gateway payments grew 76.3% to about RO 3.2 billion. In the first half of 2026 the mobile payments system handled 224.2 million transactions worth RO 8.2 billion.

03How to market the cheaper option

Counter

Put the QR where the card machine is

A printed QR beside the till, with “Scan to pay, no fees for you”, does more than a menu option nobody reads. Train staff to offer it first.

Online

Move it up the checkout

List instant transfer or wallet payment above cash on delivery, and say why: “Pay now, ships today.” Prepaid orders are cancelled far less often than COD orders.

WhatsApp

Send a payment link, not a bank account

Instagram and WhatsApp sellers can send a QR or wallet link in the chat and confirm on screen. That beats asking for a transfer screenshot.

Offer

Share the saving, legally

A small perk for prepaying, such as free delivery or a gift, turns your fee saving into conversion. Price discounts may need approval under Oman’s promotion rules, so check first.

04What to watch

  • Your provider’s actual rate. 0.50% is a maximum. Ask your bank or payment provider what they will charge for QR from now on.
  • The OMR 2 cap. Above an order of OMR 400 the fee stops rising. Furniture, electronics and gold sellers gain the most.
  • Customer habit. Cards and cash still dominate many checkouts. Growth from a small base means you are early, and early is a good time to train customers.
  • Refunds and reconciliation. Transfers are instant and final. Set up a clear refund policy and match payments to orders daily.

When getting paid costs less, the best payment option is the one you make easiest to choose.

Common questions

Are bank transfers free in Oman now?

Yes, for local digital transfers. Since 1 July 2026 the Central Bank of Oman has waived fees for individuals and SMEs on RTGS, ACH and instant payment transfers made through banking apps, online banking and e-wallets, within the same bank and between banks. International transfers are not covered.

What is the QR payment merchant fee in Oman?

The Central Bank of Oman cut the maximum merchant service fee on QR Scan and Pay person-to-merchant payments from 0.75% to 0.50% of the transaction, capped at OMR 2 per transaction.

How fast are digital payments growing in Oman?

Digital payment transactions rose from about 67 million in 2024 to more than 168 million in 2025, a 150% increase, according to Central Bank of Oman data reported by Omani press. QR payment transactions grew 133.5% in the same year.

Should small businesses in Oman offer QR payments?

For most, yes. QR payments now cost merchants at most 0.50% with a cap of OMR 2, customers pay nothing to transfer locally, and adoption is rising fast. Displaying the QR at the till and in online chats is the simplest first step.
