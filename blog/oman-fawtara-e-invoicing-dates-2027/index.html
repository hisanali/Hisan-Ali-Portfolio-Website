Oman Has Reset Its E-Invoicing Dates.
April 2027 for Big Firms, October 2027 for Everyone Else

Decision 189/2026, published in August, put Fawtara e-invoicing into Oman’s VAT regulations with two dates: 1 April 2027 for VAT-registered businesses with annual supplies above OMR 5 million, and 1 October 2027 for all other VAT-registered businesses. It replaces the earlier four-phase plan that started in February 2027. Invoices will have to be issued in an approved electronic format through a Tax Authority–licensed provider. What it means for shops, online stores and agencies, and how many weeks you have.

Updated Nov 24, 2026Decision 189/2026
November 24, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

If you read our earlier e-invoicing guide, the dates have moved. The Tax Authority’s original plan had large companies starting in February 2027 and others in August 2027. Decision 189/2026 replaced that with two dates written into law. For marketing teams, this matters more than it seems: your online store, booking system, point of sale and payment links all create invoices, and all of them will need to connect to an approved provider.

In short
  • Two dates in law: 1 April 2027 (over OMR 5 million) and 1 October 2027 (everyone else VAT-registered).
  • The old February and August 2027 phases no longer apply.
  • Invoices go through Tax Authority–licensed providers in an approved electronic format.
  • A voluntary pilot started in August 2026; early adoption is possible.
  • Your store, POS and booking tools need to connect. Ask your vendors now.

Common questions

When does e-invoicing become mandatory in Oman?

Under Decision 189/2026, on 1 April 2027 for VAT-registered businesses with annual supplies above OMR 5 million, and on 1 October 2027 for all other VAT-registered businesses.

What happened to the February 2027 phase?

The earlier four-phase schedule, which started with large companies in February 2027, was replaced by the two dates in Decision 189/2026.

Do businesses that aren’t VAT-registered need e-invoicing?

The mandate applies to taxable persons registered for VAT. If you are below the registration threshold and not registered, it doesn’t apply yet, but it will if you register.

Can I start e-invoicing early?

Yes. A voluntary pilot began in August 2026, and businesses can adopt early through a Tax Authority–licensed provider.

Can I get an exception?

The Decision lets the Tax Authority grant a temporary exception on a supported request, as long as VAT returns are filed and tax is paid on time.
