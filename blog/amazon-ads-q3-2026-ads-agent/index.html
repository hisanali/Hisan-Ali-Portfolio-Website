Amazon Ads · Earnings Decoded

Amazon’s Ad Growth Halved This Quarter.
Blame the Calendar, Then Meet Ads Agent

Amazon’s advertising revenue reached $19.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 11%, its slowest growth in years. Most of the slowdown came from moving Prime Day into June, and Amazon expects high-teens growth again in the fourth quarter. The bigger story for advertisers is Ads Agent: give it a budget and a duration, and Amazon’s AI plans and runs the campaign across search, display, video and streaming TV.

Updated Oct 31, 2026Q3 results 29 October
October 31, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

Read only the headline and Amazon’s advertising business looks like it hit a wall: growth fell from 26% in the second quarter to 11% in the third, and revenue slipped slightly quarter on quarter. Read the call and the picture changes. Prime Day ran in June this year instead of July, pulling a large block of ad spending into the second quarter. Amazon put the calendar effect at 850 basis points of third-quarter growth.

In short
  • The dip is mostly Prime Day moving to the second quarter, plus currency.
  • Ads Agent brings Amazon’s whole ad offering into one AI-first platform: you set budget and duration, the AI plans, executes and optimises.
  • Amazon says outside AI shopping agents, such as chat assistants that shop for users, are not yet hurting its ad business.
  • Prime Video is now profitable, with revenue in the tens of billions a year. Streaming TV ads are a growing part of the mix.

What Ads Agent changes

Amazon describes Ads Agent as bringing its full advertising offering together under one AI-first platform. On the call, Andy Jassy said advertisers provide initial inputs such as budget and duration, and the AI plans, executes and keeps optimising the rest. Coverage says a single campaign can run across sponsored ads, display, video and streaming TV. For the demand-side platform, Amazon calls its approach “human-led, AI-supported”, and buyers can keep the traditional interface.

1

Fewer campaigns, fewer levers

Instead of separate Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and display campaigns, the agent decides the mix. Your inputs become the product catalogue, the creative and the budget.

2

Catalogue quality becomes targeting

When the AI picks placements, titles, images, prices and reviews do more of the work. A weak listing now wastes more money, faster.

3

Measurement needs a holdout

If one agent spends across search and streaming TV, credit gets blurry. Keep a test region or period without it to see what it really adds.

4

Brand rules up front

Decide which products, claims and placements are off limits before you hand over the budget. Agents optimise for what you measure, not what you meant.

What to do this quarter

  • Do not read the slowdown as falling demand. Plan the fourth quarter as a normal peak: Black Friday, Cyber Monday and gifting in December.
  • Fix listings before testing automation. The agent can only promote what the catalogue shows well.
  • Test Ads Agent with a slice, not the whole budget. Compare cost per order and new-to-brand customers against your manual campaigns.
  • Watch AI shopping assistants. Amazon says they are not hurting its ads yet. They are where product discovery is heading, so keep your product data clean everywhere.

The quarter was about the calendar. The next year is about who drives the campaign.

Common questions

How much did Amazon make from advertising in Q3 2026?

Amazon reported advertising revenue of $19.64 billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 11% from $17.70 billion a year earlier, or 12% excluding currency effects.

Why did Amazon’s ad growth slow in Q3 2026?

Mainly because Prime Day moved into the second quarter for most countries, which Amazon said took 850 basis points off third-quarter growth, along with currency effects. Amazon expects growth in the high teens in the fourth quarter.

What is Amazon Ads Agent?

Ads Agent is Amazon’s AI-first advertising platform that brings its full ad offering together. Advertisers give inputs such as budget and duration, and the AI plans, executes and optimises campaigns, which can run across sponsored ads, display, video and streaming TV.

Are AI shopping agents hurting Amazon’s ad business?

Amazon’s CEO said on the October 2026 earnings call that agentic shopping through third-party platforms is not yet having a meaningful impact on its advertising business.
