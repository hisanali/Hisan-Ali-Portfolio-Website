Selling Off-Plan in Oman:
The 20% Test, the 3% Marketing Cap and the Permit Before the Billboard

Oman’s new real estate rules change when a developer may start marketing and how much of buyers’ money may pay for it. In the special economic and free zones, off-plan marketing waits until a project reaches 20% of its total cost, and marketing paid from escrow is capped at 3%. Everywhere, advertising needs its own permit. What the rules say and how to plan a launch around them.

Updated Oct 31, 2026Real estate law in force 10 March 2026
October 31, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

For years the typical off-plan launch in Muscat began with renders, a sales gallery and a payment plan, sometimes before much was built. Oman’s new real estate framework moves the starting line. Developers need licences and registration before they announce a project, a separate permit before they advertise it, and in the special economic and free zones a 20% threshold before they market units off-plan at all.

3%the most of the escrow balance that may be spent on marketing and advertising
6months for developers already marketing in the zones to comply
In short
  • Royal Decree 79/2025, Oman’s real estate development law, has been in force since 10 March 2026.
  • Projects go through licensing and registration first, then need an advertising permit before media campaigns, marketing channels or exhibitions.
  • The zones’ new executive regulation adds the 20% threshold before off-plan marketing or reservation payments, and the 3% cap on escrow-funded marketing.
  • Whether the 20% test applies outside the zones is not clear from published coverage. Check with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning.

Checked 31 October 2026 against press and practitioner coverage, not the gazetted text. Sources differ on the scope of the 20% threshold. This is a marketing planning guide, not legal advice.

What changes about a launch

The old playbook front-loaded spending: a big reveal, heavy paid social and a sales gallery on day one. Under the new rules the biggest campaign comes later, once the project can legally take reservations, and its budget is smaller relative to escrow. That rewards developers who build an audience cheaply before the launch window opens.

1

Before the permit: build a waiting list

Brand content about the developer, the area and past projects is not a project advert. Grow an email and WhatsApp list of interested buyers you can contact the day the permit arrives.

2

At the permit: lead with proof

Buyers will hear about the new rules. Show construction progress, escrow details and the permit number in ads. Proof of progress is now a selling point.

3

After 20%: spend where buyers decide

Search for project and area names, property portals and retargeting convert better than broad awareness. Fund them inside the 3% cap and track cost per reservation.

4

Keep agents in line

Brokers and resellers advertise too. Give them approved creative and the permit details, and ban promises about returns or handover dates you cannot document.

What is still unclear

  • Scope of the 20% test. Zawya reported it for special economic and free zones. A practitioner guide describes a similar condition for advertising permits generally. Until the text is clear, plan as if it applies.
  • What counts toward 20%. Whether it is measured by construction progress, funds deposited or both should be confirmed with the authority for your zone.
  • Marketing paid by the developer. The 3% cap covers spending from escrow. Developers can fund more from their own money, but the permit rules still apply.

The new launch campaign does not start with a render. It starts with proof.

Common questions

When can developers start marketing off-plan property in Oman?

Developers must complete licensing and registration and obtain an advertising permit before promoting a project. In Oman’s special economic and free zones, a new executive regulation also requires a threshold equal to 20% of the project’s total cost before marketing units off-plan or accepting reservation payments.

How much of escrow can be spent on marketing in Oman?

No more than 3% of the amounts held in the project’s escrow account may be spent on marketing or advertising, under the executive regulation reported for special economic and free zones. A similar condition is reported for advertising permits.

What is Royal Decree 79/2025?

It is Oman’s real estate development law. It came into force on 10 March 2026 and sets a staged process of licensing, registration and permits for real estate projects, including a permit to advertise and promote a project.

Do existing off-plan projects have to comply?

Developers already marketing off-plan in the special economic and free zones have up to six months from the regulation’s entry into force to bring their status into compliance, according to Zawya’s report.
