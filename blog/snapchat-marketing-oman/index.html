Snapchat Marketing in Oman:
The 2026 Playbook

Snapchat reaches about half of Oman’s adults, yet many brands still treat it as an afterthought. How big it really is, who uses it, which ad formats work and how to build a first campaign that sells.

Key takeaways
  • Snapchat ads can reach about 2.35 million people in Oman, roughly half of all adults, and that reach grew 14.7% in a year.
  • Its strength is young Omani audiences and daily habit: people open it many times a day to talk with friends and family.
  • Ads that work look like Snaps: vertical, full screen, sound on, people talking to camera, with the point made in the first two seconds.
  • Install the Snap Pixel and Conversions API before spending, or the platform cannot find buyers for you.

Ask an Omani university student which app they open first in the morning and many will say Snapchat. Ask a marketing team in Muscat where their budget goes, and Snapchat is often a small line at the bottom, after Instagram, TikTok and Google. That gap between where attention is and where budgets go is an opportunity.

01How big Snapchat is in Oman

DataReportal’s Digital 2026 report for Oman puts Snapchat’s advertising reach at 2.35 million in October 2025. That equals about 42.3% of the total population and 51.0% of adults aged 18 and over, and it grew 14.7% year on year.1

Like every platform’s ad numbers, this counts reachable accounts, not unique people, so treat it as a ceiling. Even so, it puts Snapchat among the largest advertising platforms in the country. For how platform numbers compare, see Oman in Numbers 2026.

Globally, Snap reported 483 million daily active users and 956 million monthly users in the first quarter of 2026.2 In the Gulf, Snap says users open the app more than 45 times a day on average, and Ramadan is one of its busiest periods.3

02Who it reaches best

Strongest

Young Omanis

Students and young professionals, especially 18–34. Snapchat is where they talk to friends and family, so ads sit in a personal, high-attention space.

Strong

Families through younger members

Decisions about restaurants, outings, gifts and Eid shopping are often shaped by the younger people in a family, who see your ad first.

Weaker

Many expatriate communities

Many South Asian and Western residents use Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp more. See the expat audience guide.

Weaker

B2B decision makers

Professional buying decisions are better reached through LinkedIn and Google Search. Snapchat can support brand awareness at most.

04Creative that works

The fastest way to waste money on Snapchat is to reuse a polished TV ad or a square Instagram post. Ads that perform look and sound like something a friend sent.

Brand name · SponsoredSame-day AC repair in Muscat“We show you the problem on video before we fix it.”Book on WhatsApp
  1. First two seconds: a face, a question or the product in use. No logo intro.
  2. Sound on: a real voice, in Arabic or the language of your audience, with captions for people watching muted.
  3. Native look: filmed on a phone, vertical, natural light. Polished studio footage often performs worse.
  4. One message: one offer or one reason to act, not a list of everything you sell.
  5. Clear action: book, order, call or chat on WhatsApp, matching what your audience prefers.

Make three to five versions of each idea with different openings, and replace them every two to three weeks. For more on short-video creative, see the TikTok ads creative framework, most of which applies here too.

05Your first campaign

Step 1

Install tracking first

Add the Snap Pixel to your website and connect the Conversions API, so Snapchat sees purchases and leads, not just clicks.

Step 2

Choose one goal

Sales, leads or app installs. Optimising for swipes or views brings cheap traffic that rarely buys.

Step 3

Keep targeting broad

Start with Oman, your age range and language, and let the algorithm find buyers. Narrow only when you have data.

Step 4

Run it long enough

Give a campaign at least two weeks and enough budget to get results before judging it. Compare cost per customer with your other channels.

Time bigger pushes around Ramadan, Eid and National Day, when usage and competition both rise. The Oman Marketing Year 2027 lists the dates, and the 2026 budget guide helps you size the spend.

Common questions

How many people use Snapchat in Oman?

DataReportal’s Digital 2026 report puts Snapchat’s advertising reach in Oman at 2.35 million in October 2025, about 42.3% of the population and 51% of adults aged 18 and over.

Is Snapchat better than TikTok or Instagram in Oman?

Not better, different. Snapchat is strongest with young Omani audiences and personal, daily use. Most consumer brands get the best results by testing it alongside Instagram and TikTok, then moving budget to whichever brings customers at the lowest cost.

What budget do I need to start Snapchat ads?

Enough for the algorithm to learn: in my experience, a few hundred rials a month for at least two weeks per campaign. Spreading a small budget across many campaigns rarely works.

Do Snapchat ads work for B2B?

Rarely as a main channel. Business buyers are better reached through LinkedIn and Google Search. Snapchat can support employer branding or awareness among younger professionals.
