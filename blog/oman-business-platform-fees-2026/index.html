Starting a Company in Oman Now Costs Less Up Front.
35,907 New Registrations So Far This Year

In October the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion changed how fees are paid on the Oman Business Platform: a small fee when you apply, and the rest after the transaction is completed. From January to September the platform handled 963,549 transactions and issued 35,907 new commercial registrations. What changed, what hasn’t been published, and a first-90-days marketing plan for a new business.

Updated Nov 4, 2026Fee change announced 3 October 2026
November 4, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Opening a company in Oman runs through the Oman Business Platform, and until now most fees were paid up front, before you knew whether an application would go through. The change splits that: a small amount when you submit, the balance when the procedure is complete. It is a modest change, but it lowers the risk for anyone testing an idea, and it arrives in a busy year for new companies.

In short
  • Several Oman Business Platform services now take a small fee at submission and the rest after completion.
  • The size of the first payment and the start date have not been published; check the platform when you apply.
  • Nearly 4,000 new companies a month registered this year, so new businesses face more competition for attention.
  • Plan your online presence before the CR arrives, so you can sell from day one.

Common questions

What changed in Oman Business Platform fees?

In October 2026 the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion said applicants for several services now pay a small fee at submission and the remaining fees after the transaction is completed, instead of everything up front.

How much is the first payment?

The ministry has not published the amount for each service. The platform shows the fee when you apply.

How many companies registered in Oman in 2026?

The ministry reported 35,907 new commercial registrations from January to the end of September 2026, and 963,549 transactions on the Oman Business Platform.

What marketing should a new business in Oman do first?

Set up a Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business and one social channel with consistent details, collect early reviews and run a small local campaign, then invest in the channel that brings enquiries.
