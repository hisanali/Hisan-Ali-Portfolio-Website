Oman · Winter Season 2026/27

Oman’s Winter Season Starts Now:
Who Is Flying In and How to Market to Them

Charter flights are landing in Salalah again, Oman Air’s winter schedule starts on 25 October, and the season runs to March. But the first half of 2026 was harder than headlines suggest: visitors were flat, hotel guests fell 13% and hotel revenue fell 12%. The UAE, India and Germany send the most visitors. Here is who is coming, what changed, how to reach each market, and a planner for your business.

Key takeaways
  • The top three markets sent almost 1 million of the 1.8 million visitors: UAE (491,503), India (382,015) and Germany (68,771), the top European market.
  • Hotels had a soft half: guests down 13%, revenue down 12.3% to RO 124.2 million, occupancy around 46%. Rates held up, so hotels protected price, not volume.
  • The season has started: the first Czech charter landed in Salalah on 12 October and Oman Air’s winter schedule begins on 25 October.
  • Market by market: UAE visitors book late and drive in. Indian visitors plan around holidays and family. Europeans book through tour operators months ahead and read reviews.

The UAE and India together are almost half of all visitors. Europeans are fewer but stay longer and are the biggest group of foreign hotel guests: 246,603 Europeans stayed in 3- to 5-star hotels in the first half of the year. For a hotel, a tour company or a restaurant in a tourist area, these are three different customers with three different booking habits.

The new season brings fresh capacity. The Ministry of Heritage and Tourism said the first charter flight from the Czech Republic landed in Salalah on 12 October with 150 tourists, restarting the Dhofar winter programme. Last season, charter flights brought 93,000 tourists, up 26%. North Al Sharqiyah has listed around 111 accommodation establishments with about 1,363 rooms ready for winter.567

03How to reach each market

UAE · 27% of visitors

Late bookers who drive

Weekend and long-weekend trips, often decided days before. Reach them with Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok in Arabic and English, Google Maps listings and WhatsApp booking. Sell “this weekend”: two-night packages, family rooms, border-friendly routes.

India · 21% of visitors

Families, friends and short hops

Short flights from many Indian cities, and family or friends already in Oman. Use Google Search and YouTube, WhatsApp and Hindi, Malayalam or Tamil creative where it fits. Show price per family and what is included, around Indian school holidays.

Europe · top hotel guests

Planners who read reviews

They book months ahead through tour operators, charters and booking sites, and check Google and Tripadvisor reviews first. Get listed with the operators flying in, answer every review, and publish English and German content for Salalah, the desert and the mountains.

Residents

Staycations fill the gaps

With occupancy near 46%, residents and expats fill midweek and shoulder weeks. Target Muscat with weekday offers, and use the National Day holiday on 25 and 26 November as an early-season peak.

04A season calendar

Oct

Fix the basics before the rush

Update Google Business Profile photos, hours and winter prices. Answer every review from last season. Make sure WhatsApp replies come within the hour.

Nov

Residents first, then the UAE

National Day on 25 and 26 November is the first peak. Run staycation offers for residents, then switch to UAE weekend packages for December.

Dec

Holiday season for everyone

European Christmas, UAE school holidays and New Year overlap. Raise rates on peak nights, add value instead of discounting, and keep paid search on brand names.

Jan–Mar

Festivals and shoulder weeks

Watch for the Muscat Nights dates and Ramadan, which planning calendars place around 8 February 2027. Fill midweek with resident offers and Indian family packages.

Visitors held steady while hotel guests fell. Winter goes to whoever makes booking easiest.

Common questions

How many tourists visited Oman in the first half of 2026?

About 1.8 million, roughly flat on the same period of 2025, according to National Centre for Statistics and Information data reported by Oman Observer. The UAE sent 491,503 visitors, India 382,015 and Germany 68,771.

When is the tourist season in Oman?

The main tourist season runs from October to March, when temperatures are mild. Dhofar also has a separate summer Khareef season from about late June to September. In 2026 the winter programme in Dhofar restarted with a charter flight from the Czech Republic on 12 October, and Oman Air’s winter schedule began on 25 October.

Did Oman’s hotel occupancy fall in 2026?

Yes. Guests at 3- to 5-star hotels fell 13% to 992,009 in the first half of 2026, and revenue fell 12.3% to RO 124.2 million. CAPA puts occupancy at about 46%, while the average daily rate rose 2.6%.

How should Oman tourism businesses market to UAE visitors?

UAE visitors often book short trips at short notice and drive in. Use Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok in Arabic and English, keep Google Maps listings current, offer instant WhatsApp booking and promote weekend packages.
