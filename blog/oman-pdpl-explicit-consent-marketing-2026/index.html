Oman Now Requires Explicit Consent Before You Send Marketing.
Check Your Lists Before the November Rush

Royal Decree 68/2026 amended Oman’s Personal Data Protection Law with effect from 7 September. Article 22 now says businesses must get a person’s explicit consent before sending commercial advertising or marketing material. The amendment also reaches processing done outside Oman and adds cases where consent isn’t needed. With White Friday and National Day campaigns about to go out, what explicit consent means in practice and a check for your email, SMS and WhatsApp lists.

Updated Nov 12, 2026Amendment in force 7 September 2026
November 12, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

Most businesses in Oman built their customer lists casually: a phone number at the till, an email at checkout, a WhatsApp chat that turned into a broadcast list. Under the amended law, sending marketing to those people needs their explicit agreement, given for that purpose. The amendment came into force on 7 September 2026, so campaigns sent this month fall under it.

68/2026the Royal Decree amending the law
7 Sep2026, when the amendment took effect
Art. 22explicit consent before marketing material
In short
  • You need explicit consent before sending commercial advertising or marketing material; the manner is set by regulation.
  • The law now also covers processing outside Oman of data about people in Oman.
  • Pre-ticked boxes, silence or “by buying you agree” are not explicit consent.
  • Separate rules on promotional SMS and calls still apply: 8am to 9pm and “AD” sender names.

Some summaries still describe the duty as “written consent”, the pre-amendment wording. Check the current text in the Official Gazette and get legal advice for your situation.

What explicit consent looks like

1

An active choice

An unticked box the customer ticks, a “Yes, send me offers” button, or a reply “YES” to a clear question.

2

Separate from the purchase

Buying something or giving a number for delivery is not agreement to marketing.

3

Channel by channel

Say which channels you’ll use: email, SMS, WhatsApp or calls. Record what each person agreed to.

4

Easy to withdraw

Every message should explain how to stop, and stopping should work immediately.

Common questions

Do I need consent to send marketing messages in Oman?

Yes. Article 22 of the Personal Data Protection Law, as amended by Royal Decree 68/2026, requires explicit consent before sending commercial advertising or marketing material.

When did the amendment take effect?

The amendments took effect on 7 September 2026.

Is a customer’s phone number from a purchase enough?

Treat it as not enough. Explicit consent means an active, specific agreement to receive marketing, separate from the purchase.

Does the law apply to companies outside Oman?

The amendment extends the law to processing carried out outside Oman of personal data relating to Oman, so regional agencies and platforms handling Omani customers’ data are affected.
