Pinterest Puts Ads Inside the Picture:
Visual Search Ads Explained

Pinterest’s new Visual Search Ads place your products at the top of search results and inside Pin close-ups, where people zoom into an image and ask “where can I get that?”. Announced on 17 September and rolling into beta across every Pinterest ad market, they run on Performance+ and send shoppers straight to your site. How the format works, the new Performance+ tools around it, and a creative checker for the Pins that have to carry it.

Key takeaways
  • Visual Search Ads let you bid for a top spot in search results and in Pin close-ups, with the click going to your own site.
  • They are built for lower-funnel goals and run with Performance+, Pinterest’s automated campaign suite.
  • Beta is opening to eligible advertisers in all ad markets; Pinterest has published no placement selector, bid modifier or report field yet.
  • Alongside: Priority Products to push chosen items, self-serve A/B testing, and an App Promotion objective in beta.

01How the format works

Pinterest’s visual search lets people tap into part of an image, a lamp in a living-room Pin, say, and see similar products. Visual Search Ads put a sponsored product into that moment, and at the top of ordinary search results, and send the click to the advertiser’s website.12

03Who should care

  • Home, fashion, beauty and food brands: the categories where people search by picture. If your product can appear in someone else’s photo, this format finds it.
  • Retailers with catalogues: Visual Search Ads are a product format; a clean feed with prices and stock is the entry ticket.
  • Brands outside the US: beta is planned for all Pinterest ad markets, which is unusual for a launch. Ask your rep.
  • Not yet: services and B2B. Nobody visually searches for an accountant.

04How to get ready

1

Shoot products in context

Lifestyle images where the product is large and clear. Visual search matches on what it can see, not on your caption.

2

Fix the catalogue

Price, availability, clear titles and one clean product image per item. The same feed powers Shopping ads and Google’s cart.

3

Run Performance+ with Priority Products

Pick the five products you most want found visually and prioritise them for the holiday window.

4

Set up one A/B test

Lifestyle versus plain-background creative, same budget, four weeks. Keep the winner for Visual Search Ads when your account gets access.

Search ads answered words. Visual Search Ads answer a tap on a picture. Your product has to be in the picture.

Common questions

What are Pinterest Visual Search Ads?

A format announced on 17 September 2026 that lets advertisers bid for a top spot in Pinterest search results and inside Pin close-ups, where people tap into an image to find similar products. Clicks go to the advertiser’s website, and the ads run with Performance+.

When can I use Visual Search Ads?

Pinterest said beta access for eligible advertisers would roll out in all Pinterest ad markets in the weeks after the announcement. It has not published eligibility rules, so check with your Pinterest account team.

What is Pinterest Priority Products?

A Performance+ feature that lets advertisers choose which products get emphasis during launches or promotions, instead of letting automation distribute spend evenly across the catalogue.

What images work best for visual search?

Vertical 2:3 images with the product large, clear and in a real setting, little or no text on the image, and a descriptive title. The product should also be in your catalogue feed with price and availability.
