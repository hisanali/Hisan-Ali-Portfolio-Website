Oman Capped Buy Now, Pay Later at RO 1,500 and 12 Months.
What Shops Need to Know Before White Friday

The Central Bank of Oman has issued rules for buy now, pay later: a regulation in February and a licensing framework in June. Shoppers can owe up to RO 1,500 across all providers, repay over no more than 12 months, and must see every fee up front, with no hidden charges or early repayment penalties. What it means for the shops that offer instalments, and a calculator for what an instalment option costs you.

Updated Nov 4, 2026Framework issued June 2026
November 4, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Instalments have become a normal part of online checkout in the Gulf. They make a large basket feel manageable, which is why stores that offer them often see bigger orders. Oman’s central bank has now put rules around them, so the service is run by licensed providers, costs are clear and shoppers can’t take on more than a set limit.

RO 1,500maximum a shopper can owe across all BNPL providers
12months, the longest repayment period allowed
0hidden charges or early repayment penalties allowed
In short
  • Only providers licensed by the Central Bank of Oman may offer BNPL.
  • Each shopper’s total BNPL balance is capped at RO 1,500, with repayment within 12 months.
  • Fees must be shown in advance in a key facts statement.
  • For shops: check your provider is licensed and show instalment prices honestly.

What it means for shops

1

Use licensed providers

Ask your BNPL or payment provider to confirm its Central Bank of Oman licence before peak season.

2

Advertise instalments honestly

“4 × OMR 60” must match the real cost. If the shopper pays fees, say so. Discounts still need approval before they are advertised.

3

Big baskets benefit most

Electronics, furniture, appliances and gold are where instalments change decisions. Small items rarely need them.

4

Some shoppers will hit the cap

Keep card, QR and transfer options visible, so a declined instalment doesn’t mean a lost sale.

Common questions

Is buy now, pay later regulated in Oman?

Yes. The Central Bank of Oman issued a BNPL regulation in February 2026 and a framework to regulate and license providers in June 2026.

What is the BNPL limit in Oman?

A shopper’s total BNPL balance is capped at RO 1,500 across all providers, and repayment periods cannot exceed 12 months.

Can BNPL providers in Oman charge late or hidden fees?

Costs must be disclosed in advance in a key facts statement. Hidden charges and early repayment penalties are banned.

Should my shop offer instalments?

It usually pays for higher-value items such as electronics, furniture and appliances. Use a licensed provider, compare its fee with the lift in order size, and keep other payment options visible.
