Oman in Numbers 2026:
The Digital Fact File for Marketers

Who lives in Oman, where they live, how many are online and what the ad platforms really count. Every figure sourced, with the questions marketers should ask of each number.

Most marketing plans for Oman start with a guess. “Our audience is Muscat.” “Everyone is on Instagram.” “Expats don’t matter for us.” Some of those guesses are right, and some quietly waste half a budget. This fact file collects the numbers that matter most for marketing decisions in Oman, from official statistics and the most widely used industry reports, and adds what I have learned about reading them after years of running campaigns here.

It is an annual edition: the figures are refreshed every January, so you can bookmark this page and come back when you plan next year.

01Who lives in Oman

According to the National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI), Oman’s population stood at 5,434,104 in September 2026: 3,074,288 Omanis and 2,359,816 expatriates.1 At the end of 2025 the figure was 5,359,557, up 1.7% in a year, which means roughly 90,000 more residents in twelve months.2

Oman is also young. United Nations estimates put the median age at about 30, with roughly a quarter of residents under 15 and fewer than 3% aged 65 or over.3 Families with children are a very large share of household spending, which is why back-to-school, Eid and summer travel move so much demand.

Three patterns stand out for marketers:4

Concentration

Two governorates, 46% of people

Muscat and North Al Batinah together hold 2.48 million residents. For most consumer brands, that coastal corridor from Muscat to Sohar is the first market to win.

Hidden size

The interior is bigger than it looks

South Al Batinah, Ad Dakhiliyah and Dhofar each hold more than half a million people. Nizwa, Rustaq and Salalah are real markets, not add-ons.

Small but specific

Four governorates, 9.4%

Adh Dhahirah, Al Buraimi, Al Wusta and Musandam together hold about 505,000 people, fewer than South Al Batinah alone. Target them only with a clear local reason.

Delivery

Distance shapes conversion

If you promise “delivery across Oman”, be specific per governorate. A Salalah customer compares your delivery time with a local shop, not with Muscat.

03How connected they are

DataReportal’s Digital 2026 report for Oman counts 5.28 million internet users at the end of 2025, a penetration of 95.3%, and 6.93 million cellular connections, equal to 125% of the population. It also estimates 3.44 million social media user identities in October 2025, or 62.1% of the population.5

Residents (end 2025)5.36M
Internet users5.28M
Social media user identities3.44M

Two things are worth noticing. First, more mobile connections than people: many residents carry two SIMs, often one for work or for calls home. Phone numbers are therefore not a perfect proxy for unique customers. Second, the gap between “online” and “on social media” is large. About 1.8 million internet users do not show up as social media identities. They still search, use WhatsApp and read news sites, which is why search and messaging matter as much as social feeds in Oman.

04What the platforms count

Platform numbers are where most plans go wrong. Ad tools report accounts that can be reached, not unique people, and they include visitors, duplicate accounts and people whose location is estimated.

Facebook alone reports 4.66 million users, 1.2 million more than the estimated number of social media identities in the whole country.6 That is not a contradiction; it is a warning. Treat platform audience sizes as a ceiling for planning, and judge campaigns on reach, frequency and results, not on the size of the pool you were shown.

Practical rule: when a Meta or TikTok audience estimate for Oman looks bigger than the population you are targeting, narrow by location radius, language and age before you trust it. See the Facebook marketing guide for Oman and the TikTok ads framework.

05When people move

Population figures are annual averages. Real audiences move with the seasons, and two events show how much.

1,070,738

visitors went to Dhofar during Khareef 2025, with July and August accounting for about 94% of arrivals.7

71.5%

of Khareef visitors up to the end of August 2025 were Omani. The monsoon season is, above all, a domestic travel moment.

hosted Muscat Nights from 1 to 31 January 2026, with organisers expecting more than two million visits.8

For a Salalah hotel, restaurant or car rental, July and August are the year. For a Muscat retailer, the same months can be quiet as families travel. For the full month-by-month view, see The Oman Marketing Year 2027.

06What it means for your marketing

Plan for two audiences

With 43% of residents expatriates, decide early whether you speak to Omanis, expats or both, and in which languages. The expat audience guide goes deeper.

Start on the coast, then earn the interior

Win Muscat and Al Batinah first, then expand to Nizwa, Sur and Salalah with local proof: local reviews, local delivery times, local faces.

Don’t skip search and WhatsApp

Nearly everyone is online, but more than a third of residents are not visible as social media identities. Search and WhatsApp reach the people social feeds miss.

Distrust big audience numbers

Platform counts are ceilings. Measure real reach and results, and compare with your own customer data. See branded search as a reality check.

Common questions

What is the population of Oman in 2026?

According to NCSI, Oman had 5,434,104 residents in September 2026: 3,074,288 Omanis (56.6%) and 2,359,816 expatriates (43.4%).

Which governorate in Oman has the most people?

Muscat, with 1,538,312 residents at the end of May 2026, about 28.5% of the total. North Al Batinah is second with 945,647.

How many people in Oman use the internet?

DataReportal’s Digital 2026 report estimates 5.28 million internet users at the end of 2025, a penetration of 95.3%.

Why are Facebook’s user numbers for Oman higher than the number of social media users?

Ad platforms count reachable accounts, including duplicates, visitors and estimated locations. Independent reports estimate people. Use platform numbers as an upper limit, not as a headcount.

How often is this fact file updated?

Once a year, in January, when new annual population and digital reports are published. Figures in each edition show the date they refer to.
