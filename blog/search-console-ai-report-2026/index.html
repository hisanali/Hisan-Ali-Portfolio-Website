Search Console’s AI Report:
What It Shows, What It Hides, How to Use It

Every site now has a “Generative AI” performance report in Search Console, showing how often its pages appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode. It counts impressions only: no clicks, no queries. Meanwhile, strange one-word queries like “yes” are turning up in the main report. How to read both, and a tool that sorts AI Mode follow-ups from real searches.

Key takeaways
  • The Generative AI report shows impressions of your pages in AI Overviews and AI Mode (and a separate Discover view).
  • It has no clicks, CTR or queries. AI data is also counted in the normal Performance report.
  • In the main report, each AI Mode follow-up counts as a new query, which is why you see rows like “yes” or “what about pricing”.
  • Use the AI report for which pages AI shows, and the main report with the branded filter for clicks.

03The strange queries in your main report

Google counts each follow-up question in an AI Mode conversation as a new query. So if someone asks about running shoes, then types “yes” or “what about for wide feet”, those follow-ups can appear as queries in your Performance report.34

These rows are not mistakes. They are a window into how people talk to AI Mode. But they also distort averages: a “yes” query with high impressions and no clicks drags CTR down. The sorter below helps you separate them.

04A monthly routine

1

Export AI pages

In the Generative AI report, open Pages and export. These are the pages Google uses in AI answers.

2

Compare with clicks

Look up the same pages in the main report. High AI impressions with falling clicks means people get answers without visiting.

3

Improve the cited pages

Add clear next steps, prices and offers on those pages, so the visits you do get convert.

4

Watch the follow-ups

Run your queries through the sorter. Follow-up questions show what people still want to know: answer them on your pages.

The AI report tells you where you are seen. The main report tells you whether it paid off. You need both.

Common questions

Does Search Console show AI Overviews data?

Yes. The Generative AI performance report, introduced in June 2026 and now available to all sites, shows impressions of your pages in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, device and date. It does not show clicks, CTR or queries.

Are AI Mode clicks included in Search Console?

Yes, in the main Performance report. AI Overviews and AI Mode data count towards its totals, but they are not separated from normal search results there.

Why do queries like “yes” appear in Search Console?

Google treats each follow-up in an AI Mode conversation as a new query, so short follow-ups such as “yes” or “what about pricing” can appear as queries in the Performance report.

Can I see AI Mode queries separately?

No. Search Console does not label which queries came from AI Mode. Short follow-up phrases and long conversational questions are clues, which is what the query sorter on this page uses.
