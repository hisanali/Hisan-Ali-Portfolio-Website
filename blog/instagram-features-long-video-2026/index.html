Instagram Is Testing 90-Minute Videos.
Should Your Brand Post Long-form There or on YouTube?

Four years after closing IGTV, Instagram is trying long video again. Features lets selected creators post horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, promoted through short clips in Explore. The test started in Brazil, is expanding in the US and may reach all US users by early next year. Creators get no ad revenue share yet. What it is, who it suits and how to decide where your long videos go.

Updated Nov 2, 2026Announced 26 October 2026Creator test
November 2, 20266 min readBy Hisan Ali

Instagram trained a generation to watch vertical, fast and short. Its new test asks some of them to turn the phone sideways. Features lets creators publish horizontal videos up to 90 minutes long. Instagram promotes them as short clips in Explore; a “Watch full video” button opens the whole thing. The target is podcasters, comedians and anyone whose audience already watches long videos on YouTube.

In short
  • Features is a test of horizontal videos up to 90 minutes, announced on 26 October 2026.
  • It began with creators in Brazil, then a small US test planned to grow to tens of thousands of creators.
  • A wider US rollout is expected by the end of 2026 or early 2027.
  • No creator revenue share yet: the main reason creators may hold back.

Checked 2 November 2026. Features is not available in Oman or the GCC. Reports also say Instagram has offered some YouTubers large payments to post long videos; we could not confirm the amounts.

What it means for brands

1

Your best clip decides everything

Features are discovered as short clips. Cut the strongest minute of a long video as if it were a Reel, because that is what people will see first.

2

Followers become viewers

Instagram is good at reaching people who already follow you. A brand podcast or a long product walkthrough can find its existing audience there.

3

Search still belongs to YouTube

People looking for a how-to or a review go to YouTube and Google. Keep long videos that answer searchable questions on YouTube.

4

Film once, publish twice

Record horizontally in good quality. You can publish the full video on YouTube, cut Reels for Instagram, and add Features when it reaches your market.

What to watch

  • Revenue share. If Instagram starts paying creators for Features, expect a wave of podcasts to move.
  • Ads inside Features. Long videos create ad space. Instagram has not said how or when it will sell it.
  • Regional rollout. Brazil came first. Watch for tests in markets with heavy Instagram use, which include the Gulf.

Short video finds people. Long video is where they decide to trust you.

Common questions

What is Instagram Features?

Features is an Instagram test, announced on 26 October 2026, that lets selected creators post horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes. They are surfaced as short clips in Explore with a button to watch the full video.

How long can Instagram videos be in the Features test?

Up to 90 minutes, in horizontal format.

Do creators earn money from Instagram Features?

Not yet. Instagram does not currently plan to share Features ad revenue with creators, but has not ruled out changing that as the test expands.

When will Instagram Features be available?

The test started in Brazil and a small US group, with plans to expand to tens of thousands of US creators. A broader US rollout is expected by the end of 2026 or early 2027. Other markets, including Oman, have not been announced.
