Oman Tourism · Going Digital

Oman’s Tourism Licences Moved Online With 14 New E-services.
What Tour Operators and Hotels Should Do Now

On 13 October the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism launched Itqan, a platform with 14 electronic services covering adventure tourism, tourism establishments, traditional art groups and caves. At the same time, Visit Oman says it now works with more than 390 trade partners in over 55 countries and more than 250 local providers, most of them small businesses. Paperwork is getting faster and distribution is getting wider. What changed, and a checklist to get listed and booked.

Updated Nov 3, 2026Itqan launched 13 October 2026
November 3, 20265 min readBy Hisan Ali

Running a tourism business in Oman has always involved a lot of forms: licences for activities, permits for establishments, approvals for groups and sites. Itqan moves 14 of those processes online. Asma bint Abdullah Al Sinani, the ministry’s Director of Digital Transformation, presented the platform as part of a wider push to make services faster and simpler for investors and operators. It arrives at the start of the winter season, when demand is highest.

Why it matters for marketing

1

Licensed businesses get listed

Travel sellers abroad and platforms want proof you are licensed. A licence you can show quickly opens doors to partners.

2

Distribution beats discounting

Being sold by tour operators in 55+ countries reaches people before they choose a destination, not after they land.

3

Small providers can compete

Most Visit Oman providers are SMEs. Good photos, clear prices and quick replies matter more than size.

4

Winter is now

Visitors arrive November to March. Licences and listings fixed in November pay back the same season.

What to do this month

  1. Log in to Itqan and check which of your licences or renewals can be handled there.
  2. Prepare trade rates and a one-page fact sheet for international partners.
  3. Refresh your listings with winter photos, prices and availability.
  4. Set up online booking with deposits by card or QR payment.

The paperwork moved online. The bookings will follow the businesses that move with it.

Common questions

What is the Itqan platform?

Itqan is a digital platform launched by Oman’s Ministry of Heritage and Tourism on 13 October 2026 with 14 electronic services for tourism businesses, covering adventure tourism, tourism establishments, traditional art groups and caves.

Who should use Itqan?

Tourism operators, accommodation providers, folk art groups and businesses linked to cave tourism that need licences, permits or related services from the ministry.

How big is Visit Oman’s network?

Visit Oman reports more than 390 trade partners in over 55 countries and more than 250 local providers, about 80% of which are small and medium-sized businesses.

How can a small tour operator in Oman get more bookings?

Keep licences current, complete your Google Business Profile, list on booking platforms, publish clear prices in English and Arabic, offer online payment, reply quickly and prepare rates for travel agents.
