ChatGPT Ads Are Open in Oman:
Should Your Business Test Them?

Businesses in Oman can now buy ads inside ChatGPT through OpenAI’s self-serve Ads Manager. How the ads work, who sees them, what they cost, and how to run a small, honest first test.

Key takeaways
  • OpenAI’s Ads Manager lists Oman as available for self-serve. UAE and Saudi Arabia were added in mid-September 2026.
  • Ads are sponsored cards below ChatGPT’s answer: a headline, a short description, a square image and a link.
  • There are no keywords. You describe the conversations you want to appear in, and OpenAI matches them.
  • Treat it as a small, measured test in 2026: costs are still settling and results vary widely by category.

A fast-moving topic. Availability, formats and prices for ChatGPT ads changed almost monthly in 2026. Figures here are from OpenAI’s help centre and industry reports as of 17 October 2026; check Ads Manager for the current terms before you spend.

For twenty years, the moment someone in Muscat asked “which clinic does teeth whitening near Al Khuwair?” happened in Google. Increasingly, it happens in ChatGPT. In 2026 OpenAI started selling ads in that moment, and since September, businesses in Oman can buy them themselves.

The answer is ChatGPT’s own; the ad sits underneath, labelled as sponsored. OpenAI says ads do not change what ChatGPT answers.3 Reported specifications for the card are a headline of up to 50 characters, a description of up to 100 characters, a square image and a link. There is no video or banner inventory, and you do not choose the placement.3

Targeting

Context, not keywords

Instead of bidding on keywords, you write plain-language descriptions of the conversations you want to appear in, and OpenAI matches them to live chats.

Audience

Free and Go users only

Ads appear for people on ChatGPT’s Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Team, Business and Enterprise users do not see them.

One practical detail: availability depends on where your business is registered, and new accounts may be limited to their home country until verification is complete. A company registered in Oman targeting Oman is the simplest case.

03Bidding and cost

Three ways to buy have been reported: reach (pay per thousand impressions), clicks (pay per click), and conversions for accounts with conversion tracking set up.3 Launch pricing in the US was premium, around $60 per thousand impressions with six-figure minimums. With self-serve, the account minimum dropped away and a daily budget floor applies instead, and reported prices in the open auction have fallen.

Read cost reports carefully. Most published figures are from US campaigns and from agencies selling ChatGPT ad services. Costs in Oman, with a smaller audience and fewer advertisers, may differ a lot. Budget for a learning test, not for scale.

04Who should test it

Good fit

Considered purchases

Gifts, clinics, courses, travel, furniture, electronics: decisions where people ask for advice and compare options before buying.

Good fit

Clear, answerable offers

A specific price, delivery promise or booking link fits under an answer. Vague brand messages do not.

Weaker fit

Impulse and low-price products

People rarely ask ChatGPT about a 2-rial snack. Social and search ads usually work better.

Wait

No tracking yet

Without conversion tracking you cannot judge results. Fix measurement first: see tracking AI search traffic.

Remember the other half of AI visibility is free: being recommended in the answer itself. That depends on your website, reviews and product data. See when AI does the shopping and AI search visibility.

05Step-by-step: your first campaign

Here is the full setup in Ads Manager, step by step, using one example business throughout: Bait Al Oud, a fictional perfume and gift shop in Muscat that wants more online gift orders before the November season.

The screens below are simplified illustrations of each step, not screenshots of OpenAI’s interface. Menu names and layouts in Ads Manager change often, so look for the closest match. Steps are based on OpenAI’s help centre and published setup guides.45

STEP 1 OF 7

Check you are ready

Before you open Ads Manager, make sure three things are in place:

  • Eligibility. Your business is registered in a country listed as available (Oman is listed) and your category is allowed. Health, finance and legal offers face extra review; categories such as gambling, alcohol and tobacco are not allowed.
  • A landing page that answers the question. One page for one offer, with the price, delivery and a clear button, fast on mobile.
  • Time for verification. OpenAI says business verification can take up to seven business days.
Bait Al Oud: category is gifts and fragrance (allowed). Landing page: baitaloud.om/gift-sets, prices in OMR, same-day Muscat delivery.

STEP 3 OF 7

Set up conversion tracking

Do this before your first campaign, or you will only see clicks. In Ads Manager, open Tools → Conversions, create a web data source (a pixel), then add an event for each action you care about.

  • Install the pixel on every page, directly or through Google Tag Manager.
  • Add events such as lead (a WhatsApp or form enquiry) and purchase (an order).
  • Keep the ad’s click reference in the URL through any redirects, and add UTM tags so visits show clearly in GA4.
Bait Al Oud: two events: purchase on the order confirmation page and lead when someone taps “Order on WhatsApp”.

STEP 4 OF 7

Create the campaign

Click Create. Choose what you are paying for and how you set the budget:

  • Goal: reach (pay per thousand impressions), clicks (pay per click) or conversions (needs tracking from step 3).
  • Budget: a daily budget, or a total budget for the campaign with start and end dates.

For a first test, choose clicks or conversions, and a fixed total budget you are comfortable treating as the cost of learning.

STEP 5 OF 7

Describe the conversations

This is the step that is different from Google. There are no keywords. Instead you describe, in plain language, the conversations where your ad would genuinely help.

  • Write them as real questions your customers ask.
  • Be specific about place, price and occasion.
  • Set the location to Oman and add the languages your customers use.
Write like this: “Someone in Muscat looking for a perfume gift for their mother under 30 rial.” Not like this: “perfume, gifts, oud, best price”.

STEP 6 OF 7

Write the ad

Each ad has a headline, a short description, a square image and a link. The preview shows how it sits under an answer.

  • Headline (up to 50 characters): answer the question with a concrete detail.
  • Description (up to 100 characters): the reason to click now: delivery, price, choice.
  • Image: square, product clearly visible; non-square images are cropped automatically.
Make three versions with different angles (price, speed, occasion) and let results decide. Try your own in the ad preview builder above.

STEP 7 OF 7

Launch, wait, then judge

Submit the campaign. It shows as pending while ads are reviewed. Once live:

  • Days 1–3: check it is spending and clicks land on the right page. Change nothing else.
  • Week 1: pause any ad with clicks but no engagement on the site.
  • End of test: compare cost per lead or sale with Google Search and Meta for the same offer.
Conversions can be reported up to 30 days after a click, so give results time before you decide.

Common questions

How do I set up my first ChatGPT ads campaign?

Create an account at ads.openai.com and complete billing and business verification, set up conversion tracking under Tools and Conversions, create a campaign with a goal and budget, describe the conversations you want to appear in, write a headline, description and square image, then launch and review results after two to four weeks.

Can businesses in Oman advertise on ChatGPT?

Yes. As of October 2026, OpenAI’s Ads Manager availability page lists Oman as available for self-serve advertising. Eligibility also depends on the advertiser’s registered country and account verification.

Where do ChatGPT ads appear?

Below ChatGPT’s answer, as a card labelled sponsored, with a headline, short description, image and link. They are shown to users on the Free and Go plans.

Do ChatGPT ads use keywords?

No. Advertisers describe the conversations they want to appear in using plain-language context, and OpenAI matches ads to relevant chats.

Do ChatGPT ads change the answer?

OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT’s answers. Being recommended in the answer itself depends on your website, reviews and other information about your business.
