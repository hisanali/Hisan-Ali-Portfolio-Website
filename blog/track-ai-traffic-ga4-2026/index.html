Where Is Your AI Traffic Hiding?
Track ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity in GA4

GA4 now has an “AI Assistant” channel, and Gemini has started tagging its links. But Perplexity still lands in Referral, AI Overviews count as Google organic, and many AI visits show up as Direct. Where every AI source really appears in your reports, and a regex builder for a channel group that catches the rest.

02Gemini’s new UTM tags

In September 2026, people noticed that links clicked in Gemini arrived with ?utm_source=gemini. Google’s John Mueller said he saw the tags too.3 One test logged 4,235 Gemini links: tags started appearing on 10 September, and by 28 September every captured link carried one. Workspace users’ links used utm_source=geminiforbusiness.4

Good

Gemini is now visible

Tagged visits show as source gemini even when the browser drops the referrer, so fewer Gemini visits fall into Direct.

Watch

Two source names

Count both gemini and geminiforbusiness. B2B sites may find the Workspace version is the bigger one.

Caution

Not documented

Google has not documented the tagging, so it could change. Keep the referrer rule in your channel group as a backup.

Don’t

Don’t add your own UTMs

UTM tags override referrer detection. Tagging links you share in AI tools can push real AI visits out of the AI Assistant channel.

Regex builder

Build your AI traffic regex

Pick the assistants you want to track. Copy the pattern into a custom channel group condition: Session source → matches regex.

Runs in your browser; nothing is saved. Patterns match the source names and domains these assistants are known to send; add others you see in your Session source report.

03Set up the channel group

1

Copy the default group

In GA4 go to Admin → Data display → Channel groups and choose Copy to create new on the default channel group.

2

Add a channel called “AI”

Click Add new channel. Condition: Session source · matches regex · paste your pattern.

3

Move it to the top

Channels are checked in order. Drag “AI” above Referral, Organic Search and Direct, or those rows will claim the visits first.

4

Save and use it in reports

In Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, switch the dimension to your new channel group. It also applies to past data.

5

Add a landing-page view

Add Landing page as a secondary dimension to see which pages AI tools send people to. Those are the pages AI trusts.

AI traffic is usually small. It is also often the best-qualified traffic you get: people arrive after an assistant has already recommended you.

04Read the numbers honestly

  • It is a floor, not a total. Some AI apps strip referrers, so part of your AI traffic is in Direct.
  • AI Overviews and AI Mode are invisible here. Use Search Console for those, and compare with clicks over time.
  • Judge by quality. Compare engagement rate, key events and revenue per session against Organic Search, not just volume.
  • Check monthly. New assistants appear and tagging changes. Scan Session source for unfamiliar AI domains.

Common questions

Does GA4 track ChatGPT traffic automatically?

Yes, mostly. Since May 2026, GA4 has an AI Assistant default channel that groups sessions from recognised AI referrers such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, with the medium set to ai-assistant. Visits where the AI app passes no referrer still show as Direct.

Why does Perplexity show as Referral in GA4?

Reports in 2026 say Perplexity is not yet in the list of referrers GA4 uses for the AI Assistant channel, so its visits land in Referral. A custom channel group with a regex such as perplexity catches them.

Can I see AI Overviews or AI Mode traffic in GA4?

No. Clicks from Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode arrive as Google organic search and cannot be separated in GA4. Use Search Console to monitor Google Search performance.

What is utm_source=gemini?

Since September 2026, Gemini has been adding utm_source=gemini to many outbound links, and utm_source=geminiforbusiness for Workspace users. It makes Gemini visits visible in GA4 even when the referrer is missing. Google has not documented the change.
