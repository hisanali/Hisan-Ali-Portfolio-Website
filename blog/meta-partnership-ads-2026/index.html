Meta Partnership Ads:
The $15 Billion Format Brands Can’t Ignore

Partnership ads, which run from a creator’s account with your brand alongside, grew more than 97% in a year and passed a $15 billion annual run rate in Q3 2026. Meta reports lower costs per purchase than standard brand ads. How they work, how to set one up, and a calculator to see whether a creator’s fee pays for itself.

Key takeaways
  • A partnership ad shows both the creator and your brand in the header, with a “Paid partnership” label.
  • Meta reports partnership ads deliver about 19% lower CPA and 13% higher CTR than standard brand ads on average.
  • The business went from about $10 billion in Q1 to $15 billion in Q3 2026. Meta is investing heavily in it.14

01What a partnership ad is

Creator video: “I tested these for 30 days”
yourbrand.comShop now

sara.runs Honestly the comfiest pair I’ve run in this year. Code SARA15 for 15% off.

Illustration. Both handles show in the header; the ad is run and paid for by the brand.

The brand runs and pays for the ad in its own Ads Manager, but it appears from the creator’s account, with both names in the header. It can use the creator’s existing post or new content made for the ad, and targeting can draw on both accounts’ audiences.2

Trust

People trust people

A recommendation from a person they follow feels less like an ad than a brand telling them how good it is.

Creative

Native to the feed

Creator content looks like everything else in Reels and Stories, so it is scrolled past less.

Signals

Two audiences

Meta can use signals from both the creator’s and the brand’s audiences to find buyers.

Scale

Your budget, their voice

Unlike an organic post, you control spend, targeting and how long it runs.

03Set one up in 6 steps

1

Pick the right creator

Match audience and product, not follower count. Small creators with engaged local followers often outperform big names.

2

Agree terms in writing

Fee, deliverables, how long you may run the ad, and usage rights. Agree a discount code for tracking.

3

Get ad permission

The creator grants partnership ad access to your business, in Instagram or Facebook settings, or through Meta’s Partnership Ads Hub.

4

Label it properly

The creator’s post must use the “Paid partnership” label with your brand tagged.

5

Build the ad

In Ads Manager, choose partnership ad, select the creator’s post or upload content, and add your link and call to action.

6

Test against your best ad

Run it in the same ad set as your best brand ad for a fair comparison. Judge after enough spend, by cost per purchase.

Fee calculator

Does the creator’s fee pay for itself?

Enter your current brand-ad results and the creator’s fee. The calculator applies an expected CPA improvement (19% is Meta’s reported average) and shows the break-even.

Runs in your browser; nothing is saved. Uses the same currency for all inputs. Real results vary widely by creator; treat the first test as research.

04Rules and mistakes

  • Always disclose. Use Meta’s paid partnership label and follow your country’s advertising rules for influencer content.
  • Don’t over-script. Give the creator the key facts and offer, then let them say it their way. Scripted creator ads perform like brand ads.
  • Agree usage rights. Decide how long you may run the content as an ad, and whether you may edit it.
  • Track separately. Use the creator’s code and UTM links so you can see sales beyond what Meta reports.
  • Refresh often. Creator ads fatigue too. Plan two or three pieces of content per creator.

Brands used to pay creators for a post. Now they pay for the right to put the creator’s voice behind their ad budget.

Common questions

What are Meta partnership ads?

Partnership ads are Facebook and Instagram ads that run from a creator’s account with the brand shown alongside in the header and a “Paid partnership” label. The brand pays for and manages them in Ads Manager.

Do partnership ads perform better than normal ads?

Meta reports that partnership ads average 19% lower cost per acquisition and 13% higher click-through rates than standard brand ads. Results vary by creator, product and offer, so test against your best existing ad.

How big are Meta partnership ads?

On its Q3 2026 earnings call, Meta said partnership ads grew more than 97% year on year and passed a $15 billion annual revenue run rate.

How do I set up a Meta partnership ad?

Agree terms with the creator, have them grant your business partnership ad permission, make sure the post uses the Paid partnership label, then create a partnership ad in Ads Manager using their post or new content.
