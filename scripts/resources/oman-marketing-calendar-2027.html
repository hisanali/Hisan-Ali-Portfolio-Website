January – June

Winter peak, Ramadan, two Eids and the start of summer

January

Winter peak
  • Isra & Mi’raj and Accession Day holidays
  • School mid-year break from 8 Jan
  • Outdoor festival season
Do: outdoor events, tourism and family offers. Finalise Ramadan creative now.
Notes

February

Ramadan starts
  • Pre-Ramadan shopping in week one
  • Ramadan expected from ~8 Feb
  • Media use moves to late evenings
Do: shift ad schedules to evenings; lead with iftar, suhoor and giving.
Notes

March

Eid al-Fitr
  • Last ten days of Ramadan: peak shopping
  • Eid al-Fitr expected ~10 Mar
  • Post-Eid trips and gatherings
Do: peak budgets in the last ten days; set delivery cut-offs before Eid.
Notes

April

Reset
  • Post-Eid spending pause
  • Last comfortable weeks before the heat
  • Pohela Boishakh (14 Apr)
Do: retention, reviews and referrals; test new offers while costs are lower.
Notes

May

Eid al-Adha
  • Arafah ~15 May, Eid al-Adha ~16 May
  • Travel and family gatherings
  • Heat builds; life moves indoors
Do: travel, gifting and food offers; book summer campaigns.
Notes

June

Summer begins
  • Islamic New Year ~6 Jun
  • School year ends after exams
  • Khareef opens in Dhofar 21 Jun
Do: enrolment campaigns, summer travel and Salalah pre-bookings.
Notes

July – December

Khareef, back to school, the outdoor season and the November cluster

July

Khareef peak
  • Many Muscat families travel
  • Dhofar’s busiest weeks begin
  • Malls, cinemas and delivery carry daily life
Do: Dhofar-targeted campaigns; indoor and delivery offers in the north.
Notes

August

Back to school
  • Khareef at full strength
  • Prophet’s Birthday ~14 Aug
  • Back-to-school shopping, last two weeks
Do: uniforms, stationery, devices, transport and tuition offers.
Notes

September

Restart
  • New school year in early September
  • Khareef winds down
  • Businesses set Q4 budgets
Do: B2B outreach, Q4 planning and National Day creative briefs.
Notes

October

Outdoor season
  • Weather turns pleasant
  • Tourist season begins
  • Diwali (29 Oct)
Do: events, outdoor dining and tourism; warm up November audiences.
Notes

November

Q4 cluster
  • 11.11 online sales
  • National Day 20 Nov (days off announced for 2027)
  • White Friday 26 Nov
Do: give each moment its own job; don’t run one long discount.
Notes

December

Year-end
  • Winter tourism in full swing
  • Christmas for Christian communities
  • Year-end sales and budgets
Do: gifting, dining and travel; lock the plan for Ramadan 2028 (late January).
Notes
