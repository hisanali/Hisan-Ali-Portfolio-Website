Case Study

Growing a Bathware Brand in Muscat Through Social-First Marketing

A 3-month campaign focused on visibility, engagement, and buyer inquiries

Social Media Growth

Instagram + Facebook

Instagram + Facebook Platform
Visibility + Buyer Inquiries Campaign Goal
3 Months Duration
Muscat, Oman
Bathware / Home Improvement

A social media campaign built to improve local visibility, attract more relevant audiences, and generate stronger inquiry intent through Instagram and Facebook.

Challenge

The brand needed stronger digital visibility, more consistent content performance, and better-quality inquiries from local buyers.

Strategy

The campaign focused on short-form product content, locally relevant messaging, and direct-response communication designed to encourage inquiries.

Execution

Weekly content themes, reel-led creatives, offer-based messaging, and structured DM handling were used to improve reach and response quality.

  • Clear CTA and inquiry-focused messaging
